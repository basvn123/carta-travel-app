# T1145: Coverage fairness (ROADMAP D6, round 2 session S61)

## Date

2026-11-06

## What changed

Carta now has a coverage parity table, generated by `scripts/coverage/coverage_parity.py` into `docs/data/coverage-parity.md` with the ranked backlog in `docs/data/coverage-parity.csv`. It covers all 43 catalogue countries, 310 regions (NUTS 2) and 6 languages. Each country is set against a fair expectation: a weighted mean of its share of residents (0.25), land area (0.25) and Eurostat tourist nights (0.5), times the 3,868 places. The run found 100 gaps, each one ranked backlog line with an ID (`D6-001` to `D6-100`) and the roadmap item that fills it: 10 country place gaps (D6-001 to D6-010, 556 places short; Germany, Austria, Bosnia and Herzegovina and Moldova hold as gaps under every blend tested), 26 region place gaps (D6-011 to D6-036, 251 places short), 24 field gaps (D6-037 to D6-060), 22 outdoor region gaps and 13 trail photo gaps (D6-061 to D6-095), and 5 language gaps (D6-096 to D6-100, the intro in English only). The 36 place lines are the D15 input for S62. The script uses only the standard library and tracked files, runs in about 5 seconds, and `--check` reports when the written files are stale.

## Research

Fair expectation source, the options compared:

| Option | What it gives | Licence | Reachable here | Chosen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eurostat `tour_occ_nin3`, nights at tourist accommodation per NUTS 3 ([dataset](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/tour_occ_nin3/default/table)) | Tourism volume per country and per region, 35 of 43 countries, latest year 2022 to 2025 | Eurostat reuse policy, CC BY 4.0 (already in `docs/tos/data_licenses.md`, rows for `harvest_tourism_density.py`) | Yes: tracked in `cache/eurostat_nights_nuts3.json` | Yes, tourism term |
| Eurostat `tour_occ_ninat` national nights | Country totals, also some candidate countries | CC BY 4.0 | No: `ec.europa.eu` refused by the proxy (403) | No, not reachable |
| World Bank WDI `ST.INT.ARVL` (UN Tourism arrivals) | Arrivals for every country, Kosovo and the microstates included | CC BY 4.0 | No: `api.worldbank.org` refused (403); arrivals stop at 2020 and count a different unit than nights | No |
| `COUNTRY_AREA_POP` in `pipeline/diagnostics/coverage_report.py` | Land area and population for all 43 countries (World Bank and national statistics, mid-2020s) | Facts from public statistics | Yes, tracked | Yes, imported (the file says "one table, do not copy") |
| GISCO NUTS 2021 level 3 boundaries | Region polygons, region area | EC reuse 2011/833/EU plus the EuroGeographics notice | Yes: `cache/nuts3_2021.geojson` | Yes, region assignment and area |

Blend: three blends were tried on the same data. Equal thirds let area dominate (Finland expected 109 places against 28 held); population 0.25, area 0.25, nights 0.5 keeps the Nordics in view without that, and nights measure travel demand directly. Each country gap is also tested under three other blends (equal thirds; population and nights half each; nights alone) and marked robust when it holds under all of them. The eight countries Eurostat holds no nights for (AD, BA, FO, GB, MC, MD, SM, XK) take the mean of their population and area shares as their nights share, so they are judged on two terms instead of being penalised for a missing third.

## What broke and how it was fixed

| What | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Andorra showed two regions | The crowding layer lends microstates a neighbour's NUTS 3 code (a Spanish one for Andorra) | Only a crowding code inside the place's own country is used; register row T1145-h for the layer itself |
| Region gaps in Guyane and Reunion | The NUTS set includes the French outermost regions | FRY regions are out of scope and left out of area and nights |
| UK regions counted twice | Region pages use ITL ids (TLM6), the 2021 NUTS polygons UK ids (UKM6) | Named regions without geometry are added only for countries placed by nearest item; UK names looked up by suffix |
| The Highlands "needed" 89 places | Area alone is the only regional basis for the UK | Region gaps are tested only where nights exist; the UK, BA, XK, MD and microstates are listed untested (T1145-b) |
| Praha, Wien, Berlin, Budapest as gaps | A capital draws the country's nights but is one place | City regions under 2,000 km2 have their expectation capped at 2 |
| Airport-tier places in the wrong region | Their `lat` and `lon` are the airport's | The city point (`city_lat`, `city_lon`) is used where present |
| Eurostat and World Bank fetches failed | The cloud proxy refuses both hosts (403) | Built on the tracked Eurostat cache; the missing figures are row T1145-b |
