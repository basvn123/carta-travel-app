## T1054: N5, sign-out and this device's copy

### Date

2026-11-05

### What changed

Sign out now asks first: "Remove your trips from this device?", with the buttons "Sign out and remove", "Sign out, keep them here" and Cancel. Clearing on every sign-out was rejected because device trips (`carta.trips.v1`) and drafts are not all in the account, so it would lose real work.

Remove does three things:
- It clears every key the cookies page lists under "your things" from local and session storage: trips, drafts, day plans, bookings, lists, crew and Ask chats.
- It deletes the `carta-files` database.
- It reloads the page.

Settings, one-off notes and the sign-in token (which Supabase removes on sign-out) stay. The list lives in `lib/deviceData.js`, and a test fails if the cookies page lists a key the module does not clear. The four strings are in all six languages.
