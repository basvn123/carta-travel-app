## Date

2026-11-07

## What changed

Two more layers run through the T1187 runner. **Lakes** (`scripts/validation/layer_lakes.py`) checks 6,211 designated EEA lake bathing sites against Carta's 2,676 lake rows: 27.5% of sites have a Carta lake, and where both print a class they agree 98.7% (1,383 of 1,401). **Mountains** (`scripts/validation/layer_mountains.py`) reads reference CSVs (an owner Wikidata export, OSM, national lists) and ships a 21-summit seed so it runs now. Nothing in the app changed.

## Research

A research subagent compared sources (2026-11-07; items marked memory were not confirmed):

| Question | Options | Choice and why |
|---|---|---|
| Lake water class and swim | EEA WISE Bathing Water Directive (per-site four-class status, DISCODATA/yearly files, reuse with attribution), national lists (France data.gouv: licences vary per dataset; DE, SE, FI, AT mostly feed the EEA so not independent), EEA WFD lake ecological status (a different quantity, sanity only), HydroLAKES / GLWD v2 (CC BY 4.0, area and existence only) | EEA register, already in `cache/eea_bathing_water.json`, no new fetch. National lists are an optional folder of CSVs (`lakes/national/`). Not independent of Carta's own class source, so `class` agreement measures the join and age, not the truth; coverage and the swim facts are the independent part. |
| Mountain height and prominence | Wikidata P2044/P2660 (CC0, WDQS 60 s limit, patchy prominence, circular where Carta used Wikidata), OSM natural=peak ele (ODbL, derived flags only), Copernicus GLO-30 (free, under 4 m, smooths spires, independent), DoBIH (CC BY 4.0, GB and IE only, some disputed rights), national lists (swisstopo, BEV, Norway: authoritative, licences unverified), Peakbagger and PeakVisor (no terms found: no scrape, no storing) | CSV loader for Wikidata, OSM and national lists; DEM already feeds `eleGap`. No scraping. Independent prominence checks are DoBIH and national lists, only in their regions. |
| Route up | OSM (circular with Carta's route fill) | Fact `route` from the reference's own flag; honest limit stated. |

## What broke and how it was fixed

| What | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Lake shore sites of big lakes never matched (Balaton) | the lake row is a centre point, 25 km from the shore | named matches reach `1.6 * sqrt(area/pi) + 2` km for lakes over 50 km2; coverage 26.2% to 27.5% |
| Kebnekaise missed at 2 km | the row is 3 km from the summit | name match up to 4 km, with `position` its own fact |
| First mountain run would have failed the D2 gate | a hard check with no baseline fails | mountains checks are soft; a seed baseline (soft only) is committed so the D2 run shows no new warnings, and the owner run replaces it |
| `test_quality_engine` failed once the results were on disk | the D2 hook warned on lakes `fact.poor` (3 items, needs 5) and on a missing mountains baseline | `fact.poor` min_n 3; seed baseline for mountains |
