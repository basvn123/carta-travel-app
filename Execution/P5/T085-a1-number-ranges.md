# T085 A1 number ranges

## Task ID

T085 (mind-map number T081).

## Date

2026-10-02

## What changed

The trip pages no longer print a price range as two prices. The cause was in the wire builder, not in the trip data. The master dataset writes ranges with an en dash ("€1,200 to €1,850" with a dash between). The builder's dash stripper, `strip_dashes` in `pipeline/journeys/build_wire.py`, has a rule for digit-dash-digit that makes a hyphen, but a euro sign sits between the dash and the second number, so the range missed that rule and fell to the last rule, which turns any spaced dash into a comma. "€1,200 to €1,850" became "€1,200, €1,850". I added a rule ahead of the hyphen rule: a dash whose right end carries a currency sign becomes the word "to". Plain digit ranges such as days and years still take a hyphen.

The same bug existed in the app's `stripDashes` in `src/lib/format.js`, which `scripts/sync-data.mjs` runs over every shipped string, so I gave it the identical rule. On the render side there is now one function that joins a range, `formatRange(low, high, fmt)` in `format.js`. `eurRange` in `src/lib/journeys.js` calls it, and every budget range on `JourneyPage.jsx` (total, per day, each breakdown row) already went through `eurRange`, so all of them now read "€950 to €1,400" from one place. Equal or missing ends collapse to one figure. Before, `eurRange` joined with a hyphen.

The spec asks for ranges stored as `{low, high}`. The structured numbers (`budget.totalEur`, `perDayEur`, the card's `eur` and `pd`, the breakdown rows) already are, and they now render once. The prose fields (food lines, hotel lines, airport lines) are free text in the source, with the range inside a sentence, so they cannot be rendered by a component without being parsed out of their sentences. I kept them as text and fixed the character at the one place that rewrites it, the build. Turning those sentences into structured fields belongs with the K1 schema task.
