## What changed

The owner answered the five open calls of 1.2 on 2026-10-13: the style guide is approved (closes T424-a, the design half of 1.2), the cheapest pin in view becomes the best-value sun marker (closes T425-a; built in the Explore migration, T427, and written into the handoff), the positive status chip follows "status, not green" (closes T425-c), the brand mark is redrawn in Lagoon (closes T421-c), and the work is pushed. The kit's positive chip is now `--text` on `--surface-sunken` with a 6 px `--status-success` dot; DESIGN.md and the skill say so. The header logo has an ink ring with a lagoon teal north needle on the surface; every app icon (favicon, 192, 512, maskable, apple-touch, the Android densities, the Play Store master, the adaptive icon and its background colour, every iOS AppIcon size) is the white compass on a lagoon teal tile, generated by `scripts/og/icons.mjs` from the tokens. The generator's token reader was broken since T191 (it read `:root` from `styles.css`, which only imports the domain files); it now reads `01-tokens.css` and follows the `var()` aliases T421 introduced.
