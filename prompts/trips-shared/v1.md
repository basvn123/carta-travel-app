# trips-shared, version 1

The lines the Trips generator adds around its pass prompts (`Trips/carta-unified/carta-unified/pipeline/generate_trip.py` and `backfill_modules.py`): `retry` heads the list of failed checks on the one retry (the checks follow as "- " lines), `previousNotes` introduces a trip's older notes in the packing and risk backfill. The pass prompts themselves stay in `Trips/.../pipeline/prompts/` (registry entries `trips-*`), where the golden set hashes them. Guarded by the golden set's self-test.

## Changelog

- v1 (T1140, 2026-11-06): the text as it stood in code at commit 55a09c9c01, moved byte for byte. The loader gives the same prompt as the code did (prompts/_golden).

## Fragments

Each fragment is the text between the two `~~~` lines under its name. `{{name}}` is filled in by the caller.

### retry
~~~


Your previous answer failed these checks. Answer again, fixing every one:

~~~

### previousNotes
~~~
This trip already has older notes, written as plain sentences. Keep what is true and specific to this trip, put it into the fields, and drop any price or distance. Do not copy the wording.

{{notes}}
~~~
