# trips-shared, version 2

The lines the Trips generator adds around its pass prompts (`Trips/carta-unified/carta-unified/pipeline/generate_trip.py` and `backfill_modules.py`): `retry` heads the list of failed checks on the one retry (the checks follow as "- " lines), `previousNotes` introduces a trip's older notes in the packing and risk backfill. Since v2 the `schema.*` fragments are the field descriptions of the Gemini response schemas: pass one's `nameSlug` and `itinerary[].places`, pass three's `evidence` and its `url`, and the critic's `checks` (`generate_trip.py` PASS_EXTRAS and CRITIC_SCHEMA). The pass prompts themselves stay in `Trips/.../pipeline/prompts/` (registry entries `trips-*`), where the golden set hashes them. Guarded by the run.mjs eval `trips-golden` (the golden set's and the backfill's self-tests, offline).

## Fragments

Each fragment is the text between the two `~~~` lines under its name. `{{name}}` is filled in by the caller.

### retry
~~~


Your previous answer failed these checks. Answer again, fixing every one:

~~~

### previousNotes
~~~
This trip already has older notes, written as plain sentences. Keep what is true and specific to this trip, put it into the fields, and drop any price or distance. Do not copy the wording.

{{notes}}
~~~

### schema.nameSlug
~~~
Two to four lowercase words joined by hyphens naming the route.
~~~

### schema.places
~~~
The real named places this day visits. Pass two may only write about these.
~~~

### schema.evidence.url
~~~
The page the figure came from, or null for an estimate from general knowledge (allowed only where the prompt says).
~~~

### schema.evidence
~~~
One row per figure: its dotted path, the page it came from, how the page gives it.
~~~

### schema.critic.checks
~~~
One row per kind of fault saying what was examined, also when nothing was found.
~~~
