# travel-style, version 1

The traveller's active travel style, told to a model in a few short lines (G12, Style Studio). The app sends the style as enum values only (`styleForAi` in `continent-app/src/lib/travelStyles.js`: no name, no free text); the servers check it against the enums (`readAiStyle` in `supabase/functions/_shared/aiStyle.mjs` and its copy `continent-app/functions/_lib/aiStyle.js`) and build the lines from these fragments (`aiStyleLines`).

Callers:

- Ask Carta's written answer: `systemPrompt(lang, style)` in `continent-app/src/lib/ask/context.js`, used by `functions/api/ask/answer.js` and the on-device model. `ask.head` and then the lines, before the closing directive.
- The day planner: `buildPrompt` in `supabase/functions/plan-day/prompt.mjs`, after the chat profile's lines. `plan.head` and then the lines.

At most one head and three lines reach a model. Without a style nothing is added, so the ask and plan-day prompts stay byte for byte what their own versions say. Guarded by `continent-app/tests/aiStyle.test.mjs`; the `ask` and `plan-day` evals run without a style.

`words` is the English for each enum value, as JSON: the lines read as plain sentences, never as codes.

## Changelog

- v1 (G12, 2026-11-18): first version.

## Fragments

Each fragment is the text between the two `~~~` lines under its name. `{{name}}` is filled in by the caller.

### ask.head
~~~
About this traveller, for choosing the one reason you give for each place. Never mention it, never change the order of the places and never add a fact for it:
~~~

### plan.head
~~~
- Their usual travel style, a soft preference. The rules and answers above win wherever they say something:
~~~

### trip
~~~
- Their trip: {{items}}.
~~~

### taste
~~~
- They love {{likes}}, and would rather avoid {{avoid}}.
~~~

### likes
~~~
- They love {{likes}}.
~~~

### avoid
~~~
- They would rather avoid {{avoid}}.
~~~

### days
~~~
- How they like it there: {{items}}.
~~~

### ways
~~~
getting there {{list}}
~~~
