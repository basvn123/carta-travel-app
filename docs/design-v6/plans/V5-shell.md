Technical audit against reference/audit.md, in the order of the brief's V5 list:

1. Accessibility. The plan chooser (`BottomNav.jsx`) is a `role="dialog"` with its own Escape listener and no focus trap, no initial focus and no focus return. The share-trip offer (`App.jsx` 1129) is `aria-modal` with no `useFocusTrap`. The DateField popup is `role="dialog"` with an Escape listener but no trap. `SheetShell.jsx` carries a hand-rolled trap whose effect depends on `onClose`, so every parent render with an inline handler re-runs it and pulls focus back to the close button. Four hand-written trap copies against one hook.
2. i18n. English literals remain in `ErrorBoundary.jsx` (the whole crash screen, and `error.message` printed in a `<pre>`), `DateField.jsx` (months, weekdays, three aria-labels, the `Select…` default), `Dropdown.jsx` (`Select…`, `Search…`, `N selected`), `Icons.jsx` (about 90 `Glyph` labels), `TransportIcons.jsx` (2) and `RatingBadge.jsx` (1 title). The calendar starts on Sunday.
3. Tap targets. Wave V4 raised the settings parts, the chooser, the close and the toast close. Left: the pass chip and the avatar on a coarse pointer in the desktop chrome (38 px, row T381-d), the header tabs at 31 to 38 px in landscape, the nav plus while rotated.
4. Phone edges. `100vh` at `10-shell.css` 10 and 698 (the app root and a popover cap). The bottom nav stays drawn while a text field in a full-screen page has focus (row T390-d). Landscape (844 x 390) gets the desktop chrome and spends 109 of 390 px on the header and the settings line (row T381-d). The bottom slot sits at `z-index` 40, under the 56 of the My trips and Account pages, so a slot message is hidden there (row T400-c).
5. Lint. 19 baselined entries in the two shell stylesheets. The detector is clean.
6. Voice. The crash screen prints the raw exception text; the maintenance screen uses its own paragraph rather than the shared ErrorBlock.
7. Performance. Nothing in the shell scripts blocks first paint; the only measurable shell cost is the View Transition on a tab change, which V4 gated on reduced motion. INP and LCP are checked against `scripts/perf/baseline_vitals.mjs` where it covers a shell view, and the result goes in the report.
8. Other V4 rows for V5: exit motion for SheetShell, the share-trip offer and the legal dialogs (T391-e); the Lifestyle fine-tune steppers still private (T381-c); the day track has no `onSelect` or index prop and sets "Day 1 of 7" wholly in mono (T397-b, T397-c); the Back control of a detail page shows its focus ring after a pointer open because `useFocusTrap` focuses it (T396-e); iPhone WebKit draws Fraunces heavier than Chromium (T394-c); an unused `dayPlanKml()` (T389-e).

Heuristics, short form: the V4 build holds the 28 of 40 the V4 plan recorded; the weakest remaining are error recovery (the crash screen), help and documentation for assistive tech (the unlabelled glyphs, the focus gaps) and flexibility on the phone (landscape and the keyboard).

Design specificity: unchanged. This wave adds no new visual pattern; every change is hardening an existing one, so every rule it needs already exists in DIRECTION.md part 7.

## Changes in order

1. Focus and dialogs (brief V5-1, harden). `SheetShell` moves to `useFocusTrap` (the hook already restores focus and keeps Escape on the capture phase; the hand copy goes). The plan chooser gets `useFocusTrap` while it is open (initial focus on its first item, Escape closes, focus returns to the plus) and `aria-modal="true"`; it stays a dialog of two links rather than becoming a menu, because both items are navigation links and a menu role would promise arrow-key semantics the links do not have. The share-trip offer and the DateField popup take `useFocusTrap`. `useFocusTrap` stops showing a focus ring after a pointer open (row T396-e): it focuses the named control with `preventScroll` and, when the opener was a pointer, marks the panel so the ring waits for the first key press. Files: `src/browse/SheetShell.jsx`, `src/components/BottomNav.jsx`, `src/components/DateField.jsx`, `src/App.jsx`, `src/hooks/useFocusTrap.js`. Check: `verify_focus_trap` passes with the new dialogs added; a keyboard walk never leaves the dialog.
2. Strings (brief V5-2, harden). `crash.*`, `date.*`, `dropdown.*`, `glyph.*` and two more keys in six catalogues; `Glyph` translates its label through `t('glyph.<slug>')` and falls back to the English label; DateField's months and weekdays come from `Intl.DateTimeFormat` in the active language with Monday first; Dropdown and DateField defaults come from the catalogue. `i18n/index.jsx` gains `tOutsideProvider()` for the crash screen, which sits above the provider. Files: the six catalogues, `src/i18n/index.jsx`, `Icons.jsx`, `TransportIcons.jsx`, `RatingBadge.jsx`, `DateField.jsx`, `Dropdown.jsx`. Check: a grep for English literals in the shell files finds only brand names; the six catalogues parse; one owner row for native review of the roughly 110 new strings.
3. Crash and maintenance in Carta's voice (brief V5-6, clarify). One sentence that says what happened and what to do, shown through `ErrorBlock` (which gains an optional second action), never `error.message`; the maintenance screen shows its message through the same block. Files: `ErrorBoundary.jsx`, `MaintenanceGate.jsx`, `StateBlocks.jsx`, `27-states.css` or the v6 file. Check: forced render error shows the sentence, no stack text, in the stored language.
4. Tap targets (brief V5-3, adapt). `@media (pointer: coarse)` gives the pass chip, the avatar, the header tabs and the landscape controls `--tap`; `@media (pointer: fine)` keeps the compact 32 px. The nav plus keeps its box rotated. Files: `60-v6-shell.css`. Check: the walk lists no shell control under 44 px on a coarse pointer, portrait or landscape.
5. Phone edges (brief V5-4, adapt). `100vh` becomes `100dvh` in the two shell rules; safe-area insets on the header (top, left, right) and the bottom slot; the bottom nav hides while a text field in a page or sheet has focus (a small `useKeyboardNav()` hook in `src/lib/motion.js` or the App sets a class; the nav and `--bottom-nav-h` follow; row T390-d); landscape gets a compact header (one row; the settings line keeps its parts, row T381-d); the bottom slot rises above the My trips and Account pages and stays under every dialog and sheet (row T400-c; the Lifestyle scrim and sheet move up two layers to keep the order). Files: `10-shell.css`, `60-v6-shell.css`, `App.jsx`. Check: the keyboard walk (viewport 40 percent shorter) with the nav hidden and the field and submit visible; landscape screenshots.
6. Lint (brief V5-5, polish). The 17 shadow literals in the two shell stylesheets go to `--shadow-1` to `--shadow-3` or to a border; the two `outline: none` become a border swap; `design-lint.baseline.json` loses those 19 entries. Row T380-c: the motion-literal rule for `src/styles/6?-v6-*.css` is added to `scripts/ci/design-lint.mjs` only if that file is shell's to edit (OWNERSHIP rule 9 lists scripts as the surface's harness set); otherwise the row stays open. Check: design lint 0 new, baseline smaller by the shell's 19; detector 0.
7. Hand-offs taken (the rows named above). T391-e: the SheetShell sheets, the share-trip offer and the legal dialogs leave in `--dur-2` through `usePresence` where the open state lives in a shell file; where it lives in another surface's file (the legal dialogs are opened from App, which is the shell's) the shell does it. T381-c: the Lifestyle steppers become the shared Stepper from `FilterControls` if its API fits, otherwise a row. T397-b and T397-c: `DayTrack` gets `onSelect` and an index prop and sets its counter's words in `--ui` with only the figures in mono. T394-c: `font-synthesis: none` on the headings plus a check that the self-hosted Fraunces weights cover 400 and 600 (the walk compares an iPhone heading with Chromium). T389-e: the unused `dayPlanKml()` goes. Check: verify_lifestyle, verify_keyboard 20 of 20, and the trips session's observer (row T397-b) can switch to the props.
8. Final pass. Long names and empty data in the pickers and the settings popovers; all six languages on the settings line and the chooser at 360 px; 360 to 1440 px, touch and mouse; screen reader names on the new controls; no layout shift from motion. Update the shell harnesses where the design changed, with a comment per changed assertion.
