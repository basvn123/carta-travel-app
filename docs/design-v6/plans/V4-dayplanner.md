The merged V3 build was walked on `vite preview` (port 5209) at Pixel 5 and 1280 x 800 with a script that counts every visible element painted in `--accent` (text, fill, border, SVG) and every mono run holding a word. What it found:

- The map spends the accent everywhere. In the Salzburg workspace 31 to 38 catalogue pins carry terracotta (the sight category's disc is `--accent`), next to the four numbered route pins and the route line. A traveller cannot tell the day's route from the catalogue at a glance. The builder map does the same (12 to 14 accent pin discs), its stay pin is a terracotta teardrop with an endlessly looping pulse, and the stay's name sits in mono in accent text. Other categories use three more hues (slate, water blue, green) where carta-design says place kind is the ink ramp.
- The list repeats the accent: the four stop numbers are accent discs, the active tab has an accent underline and an accent count chip, a tapped pin flashes its row with an `--accent-bg` ground that also shifts the row's padding. In the builder, the active "All" chip is accent on `--accent-bg`, "Show 12 more" is accent text. The returning landing's card arrow is accent. In the older sheets (13, 20) about thirty more selected and hover states are accent or `--accent-bg`.
- Nothing moves when the day changes. Moving a stop up swaps two rows with no motion (computed transform `none` on every row during the move), so the eye loses which stop went where; the stats line changes figures without a sign.
- The stats line is mono with pipe separators; the stop rows use the Unicode glyphs ↑ ↓ × and "← Back to all day plans" a Unicode arrow, which DIRECTION.md part 5 retires.
- The row thumbnail is 48 px with its number badge hanging over the edge; while a photo loads its ground is `--accent-bg` (4 pink boxes counted on desktop).
- There is no drawing of the day: the figures line says "done ~14:00" but nothing shows how the day is spent between stops and walks.
- The PDF prints in a fallback sans ("Inter Tight" is not loaded), with an uppercase letter-spaced accent kicker, uppercase tags and an older palette written as hex.

Design health against the V1 score (24/40): the V3 structure lifted consistency and minimalism; the remaining drag is heuristic 4 (selection shown four ways: accent pin, accent number, accent tab, accent chip) and heuristic 1 (no sign that a reorder or an add took effect).

## Motion thesis (impeccable animate)

Focal moment: none authored; this is an Operate surface. Continuity: a stop that moves slides to its place (FLIP, `--dur-2`, `--ease-in-out`), and the other row slides the other way. Feedback: a tapped pin rings its row once in ink; the day's figures dip and settle through `useSettledFigure()` when they change; a tab's content fades and rises 8 px in `--dur-2`. Budget: transforms and opacity only, one element per change, nothing on scroll, nothing loops. Every rule carries its own reduced-motion branch; scripted motion reads `durationMs()`, which returns 0 under reduced motion.

## The changes, in order

1. The route is the one terracotta thing on the map (brief V4-1, rule N5). From `68-v6-dayplanner.css`, scoped to the day workspace (`.day-ws`) and the builder map (`.dem-map`), catalogue pins become an `--bg-card` label with an `--ink` glyph and no coloured disc, small pins an ink dot, a picked pin an ink tick on `--ink-fill`; the must-see star goes ink; the ochre rating chip on towns stays (a rating is a measure). The stay pin becomes `--ink-fill` with its name in `--ui` on `--bg-card`, and its looping pulse goes. The route line and its numbered pins stay `--accent`. The base pin rules live in `19-guide-ratings.css` and `TripMap.jsx` (tripplanner's), so this is an override from the v6 sheet, not an edit there; folding it into the source is a hand-off row. Files: `68-v6-dayplanner.css`. Check: the walk counts accent only on the route pins and line in the workspace map.

2. Accent spent once (brief V4-2, carta-design selected state and accent marks). Selected chips, segments and toggles on the surface go to `--ink-fill` with `--on-fill` (day and city chips, chat toggles and areas, the builder chip, the ideas time segments and chosen rows, the tips add, the AI proposal's chosen stop and numbers); hover borders go `--ink`; "Show N more" and the card arrow go ink; the "Now" badge goes `--ink-fill`; "Delete day plan" goes `--danger`; the stop numbers go `--ink-fill`; the active tab becomes the `--ink-fill` selected state and its count a count chip. The accent stays on the primary, focus rings, the route and the recommended card's border and badge. Changed in place where the rule is in a file this surface owns (`13-day-planner.css`, `20-day-planner-flow.css`, `21-chat-passes.css`, `68-v6-dayplanner.css`); rules in other owners' files (the shared `guide-chip` active fill, `.daytrip-reco` in 11, `.dem-chip-*` in 19) are overridden from 68, scoped to the surface. Check: grep of the four owned sheets and the walk's accent census.

3. Names in sans, figures in mono (brief V4-3). The stats line loses its pipe separators for spacing and takes the mono size of a card (13 px is the card figure; the line keeps 12 px on a phone so stops, steps and finish stay on one line); the stay's name on the builder map moves from mono to `--ui`. Check: the walk's mono census shows no place name in mono.

4. A day you can see (brief V4-4, rule N4). A thin strip, 8 px high, full width of the list, above the stops: one segment per stop in `--ink` and per stretch between stops in `--rule`, widths proportional to minutes from `buildDaySchedule` (the same model behind "done ~14:00"), separated by 1 px `--paper`; the stop under the pointer or tapped on the map is outlined 2 px `--ink`. No figures, no legend, no accent; `aria-hidden`, because the rows and the figures line carry the numbers. It shows no clock and no leg figure, so it does not pre-empt the owner's answer on legs and arrival times (T378-e). Files: `DayPlanPanel.jsx`, `DayPlannerTab.jsx` (passes the schedule), `68-v6-dayplanner.css`. Check: the segments sum to the schedule's end minus its start, and the strip reads in greyscale.

5. Motion that states change (brief V4-5, DIRECTION 2.3). The reorder slides (FLIP in `DayPlanPanel.jsx`, rows keyed by place so React keeps the element); the tapped-pin flash becomes a 2 px `--ink` outline that fades, with no padding or margin change; the active tab's fill changes in `--dur-1`; the tab's content arrives with an 8 px rise in `--dur-2`; the stats line settles through `useSettledFigure`; the remaining hover lifts on the surface (pins, day-plan cards) go. Every rule has a reduced-motion branch that jumps. Check: the walk reads a non-`none` transform mid-move and `none` under reduced motion; the detector's motion findings are zero.

6. Photos that carry the place (brief V4-6, DIRECTION 3). Row thumbnails at 44 px with the stop number inside the photo's lower corner; a loading or missing photo sits on `--paper-dim` with its category glyph in `--ink-soft`, never an `--accent-bg` box (the loading ground of the AI proposal and Add more thumbs too). The builder rails already keep one aspect per rail; checked, not changed. Files: `68-v6-dayplanner.css`, `20-day-planner-flow.css`. Check: the walk counts no pink or grey empty box.

7. One icon set (DIRECTION 5). The row tools' ↑ ↓ × become ChevronDown (turned for up) and CloseIcon; "← Back to all day plans" takes ArrowLeftIcon. Files: `DayPlanPanel.jsx`, `DayPlannerTab.jsx`. Check: grep of the surface files for the three glyphs.
