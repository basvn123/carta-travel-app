# V3 plan: My trips, account, sharing, passes, guides and admin

T390, design wave V3, 2026-10-08. Surface key `account`. Written before the first app edit, from the brief (`docs/design-v6/briefs/account.md`, part 6 V3 list and its Direction check), DIRECTION.md, OWNERSHIP.md and impeccable's shape, layout, distill, clarify and onboard references. carta-design and DESIGN.md win over everything here.

## Shape (impeccable, unattended)

`impeccable context --target src/auth/SavedTripsPanel.jsx` ran from the root worktree and loaded PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md. The run is unattended and the owner told every session to proceed (2026-10-07), so shape's interview is answered from the brief and the code; assumptions are marked.

1. Job and audience. The hiker on a budget, phone first, coming back to a saved trip (PRODUCT.md's returning traveller) or parking candidates mid-plan. Operate mode.
2. Outcome and proof. On opening My trips the next trip is on the first screen under a visible "My trips" title; every saved place and plan carries the day cost Explore shows, or nothing when the data is thin; sharing is one visible button; nothing that erases looks like an action; the account hub leads with the traveller's own settings, not the sale.
3. Direction. Refinement of the incumbent (impeccable's "Refinement preserves"): paper, ink, one terracotta accent, Fraunces only for the page title and place names, mono for every figure. Thesis: My trips is a ledger of the traveller's own plans, one primary per tab.
4. Scope. The account surface's files only (OWNERSHIP.md), the six catalogues under the account prefixes, `src/styles/69-v6-account.css`, the eight harnesses. Untouched: App.jsx, AppHeader, BottomNav, ReportProblem, the receipt, the Lifestyle panel, every other surface.
5. States. Signed out, signed in, empty, loading, error, one to many trips (the `?savedmock` fixture holds four plans, three places, three friend trips).
6. Interaction. No new modal. Share stays inline under its card. Inline confirm only for publishing to the gallery and withdrawing a link (the house undo rule's confirm half); the undo toast itself waits for the shell's bottom slot (see item 5).
7. Constraints. Six languages for every new string; tokens only; motion tokens with a reduced-motion branch; no data or schema change.

## The changes, in order

0. Harness first (row T379-f). `scripts/verify_saved.mjs` and `scripts/verify_account_panel.mjs` assert the pre-T363 accent on the selected tab; both move to `--ink-fill` `rgb(43, 52, 70)` with a comment. Check: both lines pass.
1. One title. A visible h1 "My trips" in `--display` at the head of the panel (28 px phone, 40 px desktop per the type scale), the desktop rail label merged into it (`saved.title` reads "My trips" in six catalogues), the dead letter-x closes removed from My trips and Account (the nav closes both pages; Escape still does). Files: `src/auth/SavedTripsPanel.jsx`, `src/auth/AccountPanel.jsx`, six catalogues, v6 css. The header's "Saved trips" (`nav.saved`) is the shell's: hand-off row.
2. One primary per tab. Count chips follow the house count chip (mono 11 px, ink on `--paper-dim`, never accent); the Day plans empty CTA becomes a secondary; both "Browse the map" go to Destinations; the menu's "Edit" goes (the card tap opens the plan); a returning visit opens on Planned whenever an upcoming plan exists. Files: STP, v6 css. Check: at most one accent fill in view per tab in the walk.
3. A figure on every saved place and plan. A new optional `costs` prop (the Map App already computes with `computeCosts(data.destinations, choices)`) feeds one `--mono` line per favourite card, shortlist row (destinations) and journey card: the day cost per person with `eurDay` and `cost.perDay` exactly as Explore's card prints it, plus "national" when the bed figure is a country stand-in; a plan with several towns shows "From €N a day" from its cheapest town. No figure when the data is thin. Passing the prop is one line in App.jsx, which is the shell's, so it is a hand-off row; until shell passes it the line renders nothing (never a placeholder that pretends). Files: STP, v6 css, six catalogues.
4. Share as a visible action and the panel in the traveller's order. A secondary "Share" button on every own trip-plan card when signed in; the panel reads: make a link (scope, lifetime, Make a link), the links that work, who can edit, who can see it in Carta; "Anyone" asks once with an inline confirm (the publish button `--ink-fill`); withdrawing a link asks once (the erasing button `--danger`). Files: STP, `src/auth/TripSharePanel.jsx`, v6 css, six catalogues. Check: verify_trip_share; Make a link in two taps from My trips.
5. Destruction looks like destruction. Every erasing confirm and menu item in `--danger` (today accent). The house undo toast lives in the shell's bottom slot (T381 is building it in this wave); until that component exists, the inline Keep or Remove confirm stays and the undo swap is a row for V4. Files: STP, v6 css.
6. The past-trip form in two steps. Where and when first (places, nights, dates, name), then an "Add details" secondary opens the six folds; save is the view's accent primary; the header "Add a past trip" hides while the list is empty (the empty state is then the one entry). Files: `src/auth/PastTripForm.jsx`, STP, v6 css, six catalogues. Check: verify_past_trip.
7. The Account hub in the traveller's order. Profile or sign-in, then "Your trip settings" (Lifestyle row, value in `--ink-soft`), then Language, then Your pass (one status line and See passes, the hub's one primary), then the invite, then Help; Friends gets a hub row on the phone. Read-only rows for the home airport and group size need App to pass `choices` (shell): hand-off row. Files: AP, v6 css, six catalogues.
8. Split the Profile spoke. Profile (avatar, name, handle, email, Save changes), Sign-in and security (password, sign out of other devices, sign out), Your data (download, delete); one primary per spoke; "Data sources" renamed "Where the numbers come from". Files: AP, six catalogues. Check: verify_account_panel.
9. Friends holds the social shelf. Friends' trips leave Planned for one line ("N friends showed you trips") with a secondary that opens Account at Friends through the existing `onOpenAccount`-style door if STP has one, otherwise the line names where they are. "Alongside your friends" needs the record's counts, which only STP computes; moving it into FriendsSpoke means sharing that computation, so it stays in Visited this wave and is a V4 row. Files: STP, six catalogues. Check: verify_friends.
10. Passes in plain numbers (row T379-e). Feature lines as a number and a period ("60 Carta bot day plans in 30 days", "40 live web searches"), the Year Pass line true ("Costs less than 3 Trip Passes", from `yearPassTripsEquivalent()` changed to floor plus one, since 14.99 is 2.14 Trip Passes), the price in `--mono` through `formatPrice` (Free as €0.00), the two buy buttons named by their pass for a screen reader, the close as CloseIcon, the recommended card first on a phone; `account.faq13A` says the free plans are for the life of the account. Files: `src/components/PassModal.jsx`, `src/lib/pricing.js`, AP, six catalogues, v6 css. Check: verify_paywall.
11. One sign-in door. The sign-in modal's submit becomes its accent primary, the password fields gain the reveal toggle, the tabs reach `--tap`. Signing in from My trips returning to My trips is App.jsx (shell): hand-off row. Files: `src/auth/AuthModal.jsx`, `src/auth/ResetPasswordScreen.jsx`, v6 css, six catalogues.
