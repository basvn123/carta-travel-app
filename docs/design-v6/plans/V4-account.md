## Critique of the V3 result (impeccable critique, unattended)

Method: dual-agent. Assessment A (design review) and Assessment B (detector and stylesheet tally) ran as two isolated sub-agents; A finished before any detector output entered this synthesis. No browser overlay was injected (seven sessions share the machine's memory; the V3 screenshots in `design-v6-shots/account/` stood in for the live page). Questions skipped: the run is unattended (owner instruction 2026-10-07).

What both assessments agree on. The photo cards on My trips are the one place the surface is generic: the title, cities, dates, the countdown chip, the flag, the state mark and the menu all sit on the photograph over two raw rgba gradients (`17-saved-trips.css` `.fav-card-shade`, `.uptrip-shade`), which breaks the house caption band and makes the photo unusable as a shared element later. None of the surface's motion reads the v6 tokens: the photo zooms on hover (`transform .35s ease`, a decorative hover lift), cards transition `box-shadow`, the pass tiers lift 2 px on hover and rise in a stagger (`21-chat-passes.css`), and every other transition is a literal `.15s`. The record's memory reads its spend as a receipt without the cost bar, and its photographs as a grid; the pass card on the hub states its balance in words with no drawing. Design specificity verdict (A): the copy is Carta's, the card structure is not; a wishlist app could use the photo cards unchanged.

What only one caught. A: on a phone the 44 px menu circle overlaps the countdown chip (`.uptrip-when` is pinned at `left: 44px`); the Visited map's loading box is silent; dates are hard-coded `en-GB`; the crew remove control is a letter x. B: the detector is clean on the surface's JSX (0 findings, also with `--scope type`) but finds 3 `side-tab` stripes when pointed at the stylesheets (16-auth.css:285 `.cheap-tip`, 22-account-admin.css:794 `.adminpage-warn`, 22:1858 `.site-banner`; the admin one is a status callout, the other two break "Accent marks"); 49 transition declarations in the surface's stylesheets, all 49 literal (16: 6, 17: 21, 22: 20, the pass half of 21: 2) and 0 on a token; 3 transitions on `box-shadow` and 1 on `filter`; 87 hover rules outside `(hover: hover)`, of which the photo zooms and the pass tier lift move things and stick after a tap.

Heuristic scores (A): status 2, real world 3, control 3, consistency 2, error prevention 3, recognition 3, efficiency 2, minimalism 2, recovery 3, help 3.

## Thesis for this wave

My trips is the traveller's own ledger, so its one authored moment is the figure: the day cost that answers the Lifestyle the traveller set, settling in place. Everything else is quiet feedback at `--dur-1` and `--dur-2`: a card answers a press, a removal answers with Undo, a copied link answers in place. Photographs show the place and nothing sits on them.

Pressed state: the band turns `--ink-fill` with `--on-fill` text. Hover: a `--rule` to `--ink` border at `--dur-1` inside `(hover: hover)`; the photo never zooms. Files: `src/auth/SavedTripsPanel.jsx`, `src/styles/17-saved-trips.css`, `src/styles/69-v6-account.css`. 2. The next trip as the focal card (brief V4-2, carta-design "Focal card"). 3. Undo, not confirm (carta-design "Undo, not confirm"; row T390-e).
