# V4 plan: outdoor detail pages (T396)

Written by T396 in design wave V4, 2026-10-08, before the first app edit. It carries out the V4 list of `docs/design-v6/briefs/outdoor.md` part 6 under `docs/design-v6/DIRECTION.md` (motion part 2, imagery part 3, data part 4, type scale part 5, the phone part 7, and the accepted rules N1 to N11, now carta-design's "Detail page grammar", "Map and list in sync", "Photo track" and "Status, not green"). App paths are in the app repo and written as `src/...`. Mode stays Read inside Operate: the traveller decides whether one walk, ride, beach, lake or summit fits, then gets to it or takes it away. This is refinement: palette, type and identity stay; impeccable's "Refinement preserves" applies.

## Critique of the V3 result (impeccable critique, on the surface as merged after V3)

Looked at on the dev server (port 5206) with the device descriptors, each page opened by its share link: trail 20050, beach Es Trenc, lake Attersee, mountain Hoher Dachstein, cycle route 1150, tour de-usedom-rundweg-relaxed. Slot tops on the iPhone 13 viewport (WebKit, 390 x 844), from `wt/_scratch/T396/pre-iphone.json`: head 442 to 472 px, key facts 598 to 663, hand-off 858 to 1198, map 1159 to 1712.

What works and stays: the V3 order (decision, hand-off, depth); one accent-filled primary per page; the key facts band as hairlined timetable cells; the slope profile with its scrub on the trail; the share link for the tour now opens.

What the V4 list should fix, most important first:

1. The hero eats the first screen. The photo is 56vh (about 470 px of 844), so the head starts at 442 to 472 px and the primary at 858 to 1198 px, over one screen down. DIRECTION part 3 caps a phone hero at 40 percent of the viewport.
2. The map box is blank while maplibre and the tiles arrive, and stays a paper box with one red pin when the tiles are slow (seen on every page in the headless run). On one bar of signal at a trailhead that is the whole map. N8 asks for the item's own line or point drawn in ink first.
3. The type does not hold one scale. The trail title is `clamp(21px, 4.4vw, 28px)`, the beach, lake and mountain titles use `.bpage-name` at a different size, the cycle title a third; the hook is 19 px while the slot heads are 16 px Fraunces, so the hierarchy inverts (brief V4-7). DIRECTION part 5: title 28/40, section name 22/26.
4. The photographs hide in a fold as 64 px tab thumbnails with `role=tab` and no tab panel (beach, lake, mountain) or as small buttons whose credit appears only on a tap (trail). N7 makes them one photo track with credits on every frame.
5. The cycle profile is a picture, not an instrument: no scrub, no point on the map. On the trail the map does not answer back: a tap on the line does nothing to the profile (N11).
6. Green containers on the lake's swim verdict and the mountain's lift block (N6: status, not green).
7. The trail's highlight chips still wear the accent (`.tpage-chip-hl` on `--accent-bg`), a second accent beside the primary.
8. Rows open and close with no transition; the chevron turns at a literal `.18s ease` from the journey sheet. Hover on rows, exits and the hand-off secondaries gives no answer on a fine pointer beyond the cursor.
9. No section rail on pages of six to nine sections (carta-design "sticky section rail"; brief V4-8).
10. The initial focus ring on Back after a pointer open (shell's `useFocusTrap`, brief V5-3) is visible in every screenshot; it is shell's file and V5's item, so it stays a note here.

Nielsen scores moved only where V3 worked: visibility of system status 2 (blank map, no loading word), match with the real world 3, consistency 2 (three title sizes, two photo patterns), recognition over recall 3, aesthetic and minimal design 3.

## Motion thesis (impeccable animate)

Focal moment: the profile and the map moving together. The scrub marker is the one accent inside the figure, and the same point appears on the map; a tap on the map line sends the marker back to the profile. Continuity: the card photograph still grows into the hero (T185, unchanged), and the map crossfades from its ink drawing to the live tiles in `--dur-3`. Feedback: the row chevron turns in `--dur-2` and the body fades in over `--dur-2`; hover and press tint rows, exits and secondaries in `--dur-1`. Budget: no loops, nothing on scroll, transform and opacity only (plus colour at `--dur-1`), every rule with a reduced-motion branch. Figures that update in place: the outdoor pages have no figure that a traveller's input changes (the cost line is row T386-a, not built), and the scrub readout changes continuously, where an opacity dip on every pixel would flicker, so `settleFigure` is not used here; written as a decision, not a gap.

## The changes, in order

1. Hero at 40 percent of the phone viewport (brief V4-1). `src/styles/28-detail-skeleton.css` (outdoor's, changed in place): `.dsk-hero-media` from `56vh` to `40dvh` with a 200 px floor that never exceeds the cap in landscape, `56dvh` from 900 px (under the 60 percent desktop cap). The shared element transition keeps its target (the `img` inside `.dsk-hero`). Check: on the iPhone 13 viewport the hook starts above 560 px on all six pages.
2. Map placeholder as an ink drawing (brief V4-2, N8). New `MapSketch` exported from `src/browse/DetailSkeleton.jsx` (no new file, so OWNERSHIP.md stays true): an SVG of the item's own line (trail: the card's simplified geometry; cycle: the route or tour line) or point (beach, lake, mountain) in `--ink` on `--paper-dim`, projected with the latitude's cosine, with the place name in `--ui` 12 px. It sits under the map container; the container is transparent until maplibre's `load` and then fades in over `--dur-3` with `--ease-out` (class `is-live`). Reduced motion swaps at once. Files: `DetailSkeleton.jsx` (`MapSketch`, `PlaceMap`), `PointMap.jsx` (reports `load`), `TrailPage.jsx`, `CyclePage.jsx`, `65-v6-outdoor.css`. A `detail.mapLoading` string for screen readers. Check: the sketch is in the DOM on the first frame on all six pages; with the tile host blocked the sketch stays and the box is never blank.
3. Profile and map talk both ways (brief V4-3, N11). `src/browse/RouteFigures.jsx`: `SlopeProfile` takes `markM` (a position set from outside, which moves the marker and the readout) and `plain` (one ink area instead of the slope shading, no slope keys, for the cycle route, so the figure keeps showing what V3 showed). `CyclePage.jsx` swaps its `ElevationChart` for the scrubbable profile (test id `cycle-elev` kept), adds the scrub pin on its map and a tap on the line that moves the marker; `TrailPage.jsx` adds the same tap on its line. Distances are scaled between the profile's own length and the drawn line's, as `highlightAt` does. Check: arrow keys move the marker on both pages and the map pin follows; a click on the line sets the readout.
4. A photo track instead of thumbnails (brief V4-4, N7). New `PhotoTrack` exported from `DetailSkeleton.jsx`: one horizontal track, scroll-snap, Previous and Next secondaries at `--tap`, the position in `--mono` ("2 of 5"), arrow keys, Home and End while the track has focus, no autoplay and no tab roles; each frame is a `figure` with its photo, its km marker on the trail and its credit under it. It reuses the day track's index maths in `src/lib/dayTrack.js` (shell's, read only). Files: `DetailSkeleton.jsx`, `TrailPage.jsx` (`ViewStrip` replaced), `BeachPage.jsx`, `LakePage.jsx`, `MountainPage.jsx` (tab strips replaced; the header keeps the first photograph), `65-v6-outdoor.css`, i18n (`detail.photoPos`, `detail.trackPrev`, `detail.trackNext`, `detail.photosAria`). Check: no `role=tab` left on the six pages; verify_beaches, verify_lakes and verify_mountains photo checks updated with a comment.
5. The key facts band as an instrument (brief V4-5). Values on one baseline (a fixed line box for word and mono values), the pedigree word under the value at 12 px, hairlines between cells; nothing coloured. Files: `65-v6-outdoor.css`. Check: one row of figures at 380 and 1280; the mono rule holds (words in `--ui`, figures in `--mono`).
6. Row micro-interactions (brief V4-6). Scoped to `.dsk-page` so the journey page keeps its look: the chevron turns with `transform` over `--dur-2` `--ease-out`, the open body fades in over `--dur-2`, rows, exits and the hand-off secondaries take a `--paper-dim` hover tint in `--dur-1` inside `@media (hover: hover)`; the T184 press feedback stays. Files: `65-v6-outdoor.css`. Check: under reduced motion no transition; the journey page's rules are untouched.
7. One type scale (brief V4-7). The title of all six pages 28 px on a phone and 40 px from 769 px, Fraunces 600; every slot head (`.dsk-grid section h2`) 22 px and 26 px; the hook 16 px `--ui` in `--ink-soft`, a step under the heads and over the 14 px body. Files: `28-detail-skeleton.css` (the head and hook rules, changed in place), `65-v6-outdoor.css` (overrides of `.tpage-title`, `.bpage-name` and `.cycle-title h1` inside `.dsk-page`, which live in files this surface does not own). Check: the six titles measure the same computed size and the heads outrank the hook.
8. The sticky section rail (brief V4-8). `DetailSkeleton.jsx` mounts shell's `SectionRail` (no edit to it) with Facts, Getting there, Map, Details and Nearby, each a real `<a>` to an id on its slot; it appears once the hero has scrolled away and takes no room in the flow. A jump to Details opens nothing (the rows stay as the traveller left them). i18n `detail.rail*`. Check: keyboard order unchanged (the rail comes first in the scroller, before the hero, as on the journey page); verify_keyboard 20 of 20.
9. Chips in ink (brief V4-9). `.dsk-page .tpage-chip-hl` becomes an ink outline chip like the Loop chip. Files: `65-v6-outdoor.css`. Check: at rest a trail page's accent is the route line, the primary and focus only.
10. Status, not green (brief V4-10, N6). The lake's "You can swim" and the mountain's lift block lose the green container: `--bg-card` with a `--rule-soft` hairline, the words in `--ink`, a 6 px `--status-success` dot before the verdict word. Files: `65-v6-outdoor.css`.
