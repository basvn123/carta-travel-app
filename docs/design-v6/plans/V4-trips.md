The findings this plan acts on, merged with my own walk of the same screenshots:

1. A count-up breaks the written rule (P0). `MovingEur` in `LifestyleSlider.jsx` runs a 240 ms requestAnimationFrame count and rounds every frame to whole euros (row T380-b).
2. The accent is spent on data and on a control (P1). The climb profile (`WeekProfile`, drawn through the shared `.tpage-elev-area` and `.tpage-elev-line`) is terracotta on every day; the Lifestyle slider's track and thumb are `accent-color: var(--accent)`; the exit cards turn their title terracotta on hover (`25-feature-pages.css`); the fold header icons draw in `--accent` on the journey page.
3. Cards do not answer a tap (P1). `.jcap-card` changes only its border, and only under `(hover: hover)`; the library opens a journey with a hard swap although `lib/sharedElement.js` exists for exactly this.
4. Mono carries words (P2). "From €430 a person, 7 days" and "€850 to €1,200 a person" on the cards are wholly mono; the day's stats line puts "ascent", "negligible descent" and "hours" in mono.
5. The week's drawings and the day track do not know about each other (P2). Picking Day 3 on the track changes nothing in the week shape or the climb profile above it.
6. The length control swaps the days with no sign that they changed (P2).

## The changes, in order

1. Figures that settle in place, not count (T380-b; brief V4.5). `MovingEur` becomes a figure that changes at once, set to the cent through `Intl.NumberFormat` with two decimals, and dips once through `useSettledFigure()` from `src/lib/figureChange.js` (no-op under reduced motion). The slider's trade-off sentence and the party line keep the same formatter, so no line on the receipt shows a rounded twin of a figure set to the cent. Files: `src/browse/LifestyleSlider.jsx`, `src/browse/JourneyPage.jsx`. Check: moving the slider changes the total with no intermediate figure (Playwright reads the text twice within 100 ms of a change).
2. Spend the accent once (brief V4.3). The Lifestyle slider's track and thumb to `--ink-fill` (the selected-state colour), focus ring unchanged; the climb profile drawn in the ink ramp on the journey page, with only the selected day in `--accent`; the fold header icons on the journey page to `--ink-soft`; the exit cards' hover to an ink underline instead of a terracotta title. Files: `src/styles/35-lifestyle-slider.css` (in place), `src/styles/25-feature-pages.css` (the `.jpage-exit` hover, in place), `src/styles/66-v6-trips.css` (scoped overrides for shared classes this surface does not own). Check: a phone screenshot of the page's first two screens shows terracotta only on the focus ring, the selected day and the primary.
3. One selected day across the week (brief V4.1, as far as this surface's files reach). The day in view on the day track is the selected day: its effort bar in the week shape and its column in the climb profile are the one `--accent` item, and every day number under the week shape is a `--tap` button that brings that day onto the track. The track's index is read from the track itself (its dots), because `DayTrack.jsx` is shell's and has no `onSelect` yet; the map of the week is not built (see below). Files: `src/browse/JourneyPage.jsx`, `src/browse/WeekPlan.jsx`, `src/browse/JourneyRoute.jsx`, `66-v6-trips.css`, i18n. Check: on the hut circuit, Next on the track moves the accent to day 2 in both drawings; tapping day 5 under the week shape brings Day 5 onto the track; verify_keyboard stays 20 of 20.
