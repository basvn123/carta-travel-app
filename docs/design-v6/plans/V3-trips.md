## Shape (impeccable, unattended)

Impeccable context was run once from the root worktree with the app file as target. It resolved the project root to the app worktree, so it reported no PRODUCT.md and no DESIGN.md (they live at the repo root, which the app tree does not contain) and asked for init. That is a path artefact, not a missing context: PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md and the carta-design skill were read directly, and init was not run. The AUTONOMY_DIRECTIVE_CHECK is answered by the owner's instruction of 2026-10-07 to proceed without asking; no decision page, live mode or question tool is opened. Where shape would ask, the plan takes the option that keeps Carta's palette, type and identity. Mode stays Operate with Read inside it, as the brief chose.

1. Job and audience: one budget traveller, hiker first, choosing one written week of 253 on a phone and preparing it over several evenings, often on a laptop. Visitor mode: Operate (choose), then Read (prepare).
2. Outcome and proof: a week they can afford at their Lifestyle, in their month, with the price on the first screen of the list and of the page, the trip's own receipt as the cost, and a way to send the week to themselves. Proof is the trip's own authored budget with its est marks, never a nearby town's receipt standing in for it.
3. Direction: the incumbent world untouched (paper, ink, one terracotta, Fraunces names, mono figures, the T362 components). Structural thesis: the library is a priced list, not a magazine; the page is a plan with a receipt, in the order fit, cost, getting there, the week, sleep, prepare, risks, as seven groups rather than eighteen equal folds.
4. Scope: V3 items 1 to 12 of the brief, inside this surface's files. Anti-goals: no new colour, no gradient, no text on a photograph, no second Lifestyle control, no flight figure of Carta's, no generated sentence that disagrees with the authored week, no autoplay.
5. States: 253 journeys in 10 styles (`public/journeys/index.json`); a style holds 5 to 33 trips; loading, load error and empty states on the grid, the list and the page.
6. Interaction: the length is one segmented control (short first, as on the page); everything else that narrows the list is in one Filters sheet with its summary on the button; the page opens on title, photo, strip, then the decision lines.
7. Open decisions not invented here: a compare of weeks (T376-d), the week map (V4), anything in another surface's file (hand-off rows below).
