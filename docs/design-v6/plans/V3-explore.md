# V3 plan: Explore, the map tab (T383)

Written by T383 (design wave V3, 2026-10-08) before the first app edit, from `docs/design-v6/briefs/explore.md` (part 6, V3 list, and its Direction check), `docs/design-v6/DIRECTION.md`, `docs/design-v6/OWNERSHIP.md`, the carta-design skill and impeccable's shape, layout, distill, clarify and onboard references.

## Shape, run on the surface as it is after the V2 merge

Impeccable context was run from the root worktree with `--target` on the app's `src/browse/ExploreTab.jsx`. As in V1 (row T372-g) it resolved the project root to the app repo and reported NO_PRODUCT_MD and BUILD_INIT_REQUIRED; PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md were read directly and init was not run. The run is unattended and the owner told every session to proceed (2026-10-07), so shape's discovery interview is answered from the brief and the research, and every open choice takes the option that keeps Carta's palette, type and identity. V2 changed nothing a traveller sees (it added tokens, `settleFigure()` and the empty v6 sheets), so the surface is the one the V1 walk recorded.

Job and audience. A budget traveller with no destination yet, on a phone first (the sofa, the commute), comparing many towns to keep two to four. Mode: Operate.

Outcome and proof. Within the first phone screen they see what a day costs at their Lifestyle on every card, how many places fit their inputs, and the inputs themselves (Lifestyle, ceiling, month, car-free). Proof is Carta's own: a mono figure on every card with its pedigree word, one count that agrees with every list on the page.

Direction. The incumbent world, untouched. Structural thesis: inputs on top, a count, then priced places; the map is the same list in space. Focal moment: the first cost-led rail, cheapest first.

Scope. The V3 list of the brief (structure, information, flow). Visual and motion work (popover preview, accent spending, card ratios, chip motion) is V4; tap targets, names and failure states are V5.

Anti-goals. No new colour, no gradient, no hero photograph, no scroll-triggered motion, no second Lifestyle control, no fare presented as Carta's own (since 2026-10-21, T517, the Explore card shows Google Flights' price, named as theirs), no edit outside the files OWNERSHIP.md gives to explore.

Assumptions taken without an interview. The settings line (T381) runs in the same wave and is invisible in this branch, so Explore keeps its own Lifestyle door and month select as the fallback (row T380-e). The glossary is the shell's, so the InfoDots on "national" and on the four role words wait for the shell to add the terms; InfoDot renders nothing for an unknown term, so the dot can be wired now and lights up when the term lands.

## The changes, in order

1. Live count in the control bar. The h1 stays "Explore Europe" and a count line under it reads "3,855 places" (or "12 in view" on the map) in `--mono`, from `gridTotal`, visible at both widths; on a phone the count and the sort share one row. Files: ExploreTab.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css, six i18n catalogues (`explore.count`, `explore.countMap`). Check: the count equals the cards the sentinel can reach (verify_explore gains the assertion).
2. One count everywhere. TierLegend counts the rows the grid and rails count (ExploreTab passes `rows`), so the key's top tier equals the top rail's N. Files: TierLegend.jsx, ExploreTab.jsx.
3. The input row. Under the title line one band holds, in order: the Lifestyle door (fallback until the settings line lands), "Up to €N a day" (Any, 40, 60, 80, 100, 130; URL `xb`), the month (Any or one of twelve; URL `xm`, moved out of the Practical fold), No car needed (moved out of the Practical fold, same `xp=c` flag) and the shortlist as a labelled chip ("Shortlist 2"). The fixed "Under €70 a day" toggle is removed; an old `xp=e` link maps to a €70 ceiling, which the select shows as its own option. On a phone the band scrolls sideways as one row at `--tap` height. Secondary styling, no accent tint on the Lifestyle door (explore N1 as amended). Files: ExploreTab.jsx, ExploreFilterRail.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css, i18n. Check: each input changes the grid and the count, survives a reload, and an `xp=e` link filters at €70.
4. Phone chrome to a third of the screen. Search and Filters share one row; the trip-style strip leaves the phone toolbar and the desktop panel and becomes the first fold of the filter rail (so it is in the phone sheet too); the shortlist moves into the input row; the h1 is visible with the count. Target: the first priced card's top at or under 300 px at 380 x 800 (490 px in V1). Files: ExploreTab.jsx, CategoryRail.jsx, ExploreFilterRail.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css.
5. Price on every card. Rail cards get the cost line; on the grid card the euro figure moves to line 1 opposite the name and score, the gauge stays on line 2. Files: ExploreRails.jsx, ExploreTab.jsx, CostSummary.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css. Check: every rail and grid card at both widths shows a euro figure.
6. Lead with cost (explore N8). The first rail is "Under €N a day in <month>": tier 1 and up, best months include the chosen or current month, under the ceiling or (without one) the cheapest third, sorted by day cost, at most two per country among the first twelve so it still rises left to right. Its "See all" sets the ceiling, the month and the cost sort. The top-tier rail moves second, gems third; the month rail becomes a door. Files: ExploreTab.jsx, ExploreRails.jsx, i18n.
7. Trip styles are filters like the others: chips, the Filters badge, Clear all and the empty state's reset all include them. "Kind of place" becomes "Size of place", closed by default. The InfoDot per role word needs glossary terms the shell owns: register row. Files: ExploreTab.jsx, ExploreFilterRail.jsx, i18n.
8. Pedigree on the card (explore N3). When the bed or food figure is a country-level stand-in, the card's cost line carries the word "national" in `--ui` after the figure, with an InfoDot wired for when the shell adds the term. On touch the preview, with its compact receipt, opens from the info control, which becomes visible on a coarse pointer; the price itself sits inside the card's open-the-page button, and a button inside a button is invalid, so "tap on the price" is met by the control beside it (recorded as a deviation). Files: CostSummary.jsx, ExploreTab.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css, i18n.
9. Price pins and the cheapest pin (explore N4). From zoom 7 the keyboard layer's pins (at most 30) render as labels, name in `--ui` 12 px and the day cost in `--mono` 12 px on `--bg-card` with a `--rule` border and 6 px radius; the cheapest in view is `--accent` with `--on-fill`. Other pins stay dots. Tips, pin cards and pin labels for screen readers add the day cost. Clusters lose the ochre stroke for `--ink-mute`. Files: ExploreMap.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css, i18n. `pinKeys.js` and `tokenColors.js` are the shell's and are not needed (`--ink-mute` is already mirrored).
10. Map and list in sync (explore N5). On desktop a card under the pointer or in focus rings its pin 2 px `--ink`; a pin under the pointer or in focus outlines its card 2 px `--ink` and scrolls it into view unless the list moved in the last second. On a phone the map view takes the height under the input band to the nav (100dvh based). The brief's bottom drawer for the phone list is a new interaction with no written rule beyond "the list in the bottom drawer" in DIRECTION part 7; it is built only if time allows, otherwise a register row for V5 adapt. Files: ExploreTab.jsx, ExploreMap.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css.
11. Empty states that name the cause. The empty line names the last filter set ("Nothing matches Up to €40 a day") and offers "Remove <that filter>" with "Clear all" as a secondary; the shortlist view with no stars offers "Show all places". The old "widen the price" copy goes. Files: ExploreTab.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css, i18n (`results.*`, `explore.*`).
12. Returning visit. The bare "?" pip becomes the words "Score key"; the input row shows Lifestyle, ceiling and month without opening anything. The tier key's "×" close and the search field's "×" become CloseIcon (close control rule), and the "→" arrows on the country banner and rows become the chevron icon. Files: TierLegend.jsx, ExploreTab.jsx, ExploreFilterRail.jsx, i18n (`legend.*` is the shell's, so the new label goes under `explore.*`).
13. The floating List/Map switch on a phone stops covering the last card: the feed reserves its height at the end. File: 62-v6-explore.css.
