Written 2026-10-08 by T392, design wave V4, in planning mode before any app edit. It carries out the V4 list of `docs/design-v6/briefs/landing.md` (part 6) as amended by its Direction check and by `docs/design-v6/DIRECTION.md` (parts 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7), on the surface as merged after V3 (root `main` c8660d8d3, app `master` e2d9e1e). ## Critique of the V3 result

Method: dual-agent (Assessment A, a design review from the source and the V3 screenshots; Assessment B, the detector and a browser overlay on the dev server at port 5202), run as two isolated subagents; this session synthesised. Impeccable context ran from the root worktree with `--target ../T392-app/src/browse/LandingPage.jsx`; as in V3 it resolved the app repo as the project root and found no PRODUCT.md or DESIGN.md there, so both were read directly. Questions skipped: the owner told every session of this run to proceed without asking (2026-10-07).

Verdict: specific to Carta (a receipt where a hero would be, mono figures with their tilde and "est.", provenance in words), not generic. Heuristics, from A: status 3, real world 3, control 3, consistency 2, error prevention 3, recognition 4, aesthetic 3 on desktop and 2 on a phone, help 2. Detector (B): 0 findings on the three JSX files; the in-page overlay found two false positives (the primary's padding, which is 44 px by height; an 11 px screen-reader-only span) and one real finding, desktop line length of 91 to 100 characters on the instrument line and the coverage paragraphs. Browser facts (B): ten font sizes in the page, Fraunces at 22, 26, 28 and 40 px, the sum at 30 and 34 px, three 32 px selects on a phone, the `frr-reprice` keyframe at a literal 0.2 s on three figures.

Priority issues this plan takes: (P0) the phone fold fails in the visible viewport of a real phone (iPhone 13: sum 548 to 619 against a nav top of 578, primary under the nav), and the harness measures only 380 x 800; (P1) the title promises what a day costs per person and the biggest figure is a seven-night total for two, with no per-person-per-day figure anywhere; (P1) the reprice is not the house primitive (a literal keyframe on a remounted list); (P3) the receipt head at 22 px against the scale's 15 px, and the long desktop measure. Taken elsewhere or left: the priced list's rows as buttons (T382-a, waits on destination URLs), the scrim literal and the 11 px est. tag (brief V5-5), Fraunces looking heavier in WebKit (a font loading check for the shell, which owns the faces), "est. national" glossary entries (T099-e, shell), the demo default stay and a measured-only list (product calls, raised as rows if not already open).

## Direction for this wave

Refinement, not redesign (impeccable's "Refinement preserves"): the palette, the three faces, the receipt and the V3 order stay. Mode: Persuade on the landing page, Operate inside the first-run receipt. Dynamic means the page answers the visitor's input: the receipt's figures settle in place when an input changes, the cost bar redraws, the priced list's figures settle, a proof point's specimen follows the town. Nothing moves on its own, on scroll or on load.

Motion thesis (impeccable animate). Focal moment: the reprice, one opacity dip of every figure that changed through `settleFigure()` (`src/lib/figureChange.js`, `--dur-2`, `--ease-out`), the same primitive the trip planner and Explore use. Continuity: the receipt carrying into the destination page's receipt (rule R3) needs the shared-element primitive to accept a non-image element, which is the shell's file; written up as open. Feedback: the existing touch feedback (`02-base.css`), a `--paper-dim` hover on the strip cells under `(hover: hover)`, focus on every control. Budget: one Web Animation per changed figure, at most nine on a reprice.

## The changes, in order

Each item names the files, the impeccable command whose reference guides it, and how it is checked.

1. One reprice motion (brief V4-1, rule R6). ReceiptCard stops remounting its lines on every total (the `key={total}` remount and the `frr-reprice` keyframe in `38-first-run.css` go) and instead calls `settleFigure()` on each figure whose value changed: the line figures and the sum. The priced list's figures settle the same way when the party or the stay changes them. Reduced motion: `settleFigure` is a no-op there (it asks `prefersReducedMotion()`), so the figures change at once. Files: `src/components/ReceiptCard.jsx`, `src/browse/LandingPage.jsx`, `src/styles/38-first-run.css`. Command: animate. Check: no `@keyframes` left on the surface; the harness reads the Web Animations on a figure after a reprice and none under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

2. The cost bar above the receipt lines (brief V4-2, carta-design "The cost bar"). ReceiptCard takes the shell's `CostBar` as it is, one segment per receipt line in receipt order, weight equal to the line's euros, the ink ramp, no accent, no legend, no figure on the bar; both receipts pass their lines, so the destination and journey pages get it too (additive). The bar redraws on a reprice because its weights are the lines'. Files: `ReceiptCard.jsx`, `src/styles/61-v6-landing.css` (its place inside the card only). Command: bolder with `reference/visualize.md`. Check: segment count and order equal the lines', each segment's share of the track equals its line's share of the total within 1 px (harness).

3. Proof points that point (brief V4-3, rule R8). Each of the three proof points carries one live fragment of the receipt above it, set exactly as the receipt sets it (the same line component, no added emphasis): "It names its source" the stay line with its provenance rows; "It marks what is estimated" the sum when the total holds an estimate, or one plain sentence when every line of the current town is measured (Porto), never a typed example; "It stays the same on every screen" the food line, whose per-day figure is the one the Explore cost index holds for that town (checked: 57.76, 61.61 and 38.63 on both for Edinburgh, Chamonix and Porto at three parties). The brief's "total beside the Explore figure" is not built: the Explore card prints the bed at a different basis and rounds to whole euros, so the two figures would disagree on the screen that claims they agree (open row). Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, `ReceiptCard.jsx` (export the line and sum components), `61-v6-landing.css`, six catalogues (one new key). Command: bolder. Check: each specimen's figure equals the receipt's figure for the same line (harness), and changes when the town changes.

4. The town picker as places (brief V4-4). Not built: rule R2 is rejected in DIRECTION.md part 9 (the receipt is the landing's picture, part 3). The toggles keep the R7 metadata line.

5. Landing to town page as one motion (brief V4-5, rule R3). Not built in this wave: `openShared()` in `src/lib/sharedElement.js` names only an `img`, and that file is the shell's. A register row asks the shell to accept a named non-image element; then `LandingPage.jsx` names its receipt card and `FirstRunReceipt.jsx` claims it with `claimShared()`, both already this surface's files.

6. Type for the fold and the hierarchy (brief V4-6, DIRECTION part 5). The receipt head at the scale's 15 px (`.frr-title` 22 px today); the landing's section headings at 22 px on a phone and 26 px from 769 px (`.lp-h2` is 26 px on a phone today, `.lp-walk-title` 22 px everywhere); one sum size on both receipts (already one component); no mono label (already). Files: `38-first-run.css`, `39-landing.css`, `61-v6-landing.css`. Command: typeset. Check: every Fraunces size inside `.lp-page` and `.frr` is one of 15, 22, 26, 28, 40 px (harness).

7. The phone fold, levers that have a written rule (register row T382-b). DIRECTION part 6 orders the Persuade page as claim, receipt, primary, then the running instrument line; and part 7 says that when the primary cannot make the first screen, the content above it is shortened. So: the instrument line moves from under the title to under the primary at every width (DOM order stays reading order), and the "Price a stay in" label is visually hidden below 769 px (it stays the group's accessible name). Together they return about 68 px on a phone, about what the cost bar costs. Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, `61-v6-landing.css`. Command: layout. Check: positions on the five devices before and after (report).

8. Coverage as a picture (brief V4-7, rule R4). Each coverage row carries one thin bar, at most 8 px, under the "Regions with none" cell: regions with at least one row filled in `--ink-soft`, regions with none in `--rule`, against the regions where the layer applies; `aria-hidden`, because the cell says the same in words; no accent, no legend. Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, `61-v6-landing.css`. Command: bolder with `reference/visualize.md`. Check: each bar's filled share equals (applies minus empty) over applies (harness), and the table reads the same without it.

9. Touch and hover feedback (brief V4-8). The town toggles and the strip cells are buttons and labels, so the shared touch feedback in `02-base.css` already answers a press; this adds a `--paper-dim` hover on the strip cells under `(hover: hover)`, a focus ring on the cell around a focused select (as the first-run strip does), and the colour transitions at `--dur-1` from the tokens (the literal-free form) with their reduced-motion branch. Files: `61-v6-landing.css`, `39-landing.css` (retire the old untimed transition). Command: delight. Check: hover, focus, active on every control (quality floor run).

10. A receipt-shaped loading state (brief V4-9). When the page has data but no priceable demo town yet, the hero draws the strip's shape and a still `--paper-dim` receipt block of the final height, `aria-busy` with "Loading" for screen readers, so the primary does not move when the receipt arrives. No shimmer (DIRECTION part 2.3, Loading). Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, `61-v6-landing.css`. Command: delight. Check: the skeleton's block takes the receipt's min-height on a phone, so the primary does not move when the figures arrive; a throttled first visit is observed in the browser and the result written in the report.

11. Two additive props on FirstRunReceipt for the trip page (register row T387-c, raised by trips V3 for this wave): `hideStay` drops the Stay cell (a nearby town's receipt on a trip page never carries its own stay select, carta-design "Receipt, extended"), and `seedMonth` (1 to 12) seeds Carta's default dates in that month while the visitor has not chosen dates, so "Set your dates" and the orientation still treat them as Carta's. Trips passes them in its V4 session (T397); nothing changes on a page that does not pass them. Files: `FirstRunReceipt.jsx`. Check: unit test for the seeded window.

12. The day figure the title promises (critique P1). On the landing receipt the per-person line becomes the per-person-per-day figure ("~ €182.61 a person a day"), the total divided by the party and the nights, rounded to the cent, with its tilde under an estimate; no new line, so the fold pays nothing. The first-run receipt keeps "each". Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, six catalogues (one key). Check: the harness recomputes it from the total.

13. The measure (detector finding). The instrument line and the coverage paragraphs cap at a measure in `ch` that holds 65 to 75 characters on desktop. Files: `61-v6-landing.css`.

14. The harness measures the real first screen (critique P0). `verify_landing.mjs` gains the visible-viewport fold on an iPhone 13 sized phone (390 x 664) as an INFO line with the positions, alongside the existing 380 x 800 check, plus the new checks of items 1, 2, 3, 6, 8 and 12. It reports the iPhone fold rather than failing on it, because no rule-backed lever left closes it (report).

Register rows read and left as they are, with the reason in the report: T382-i (whether a length chosen on the landing counts as chosen dates is a product call, and the flag would live in the shell's date state), T381-g and T382-c (the raised plus over the content is the shell's bar), T382-d (tap targets, brief V5-1), T209-c (prerender, brief V5-6).
