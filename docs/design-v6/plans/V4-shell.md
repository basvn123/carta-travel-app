Assessment A (design review) and Assessment B (detector and design lint) ran as two isolated sub-agents; A read the source and eleven screenshots of the current build (iPhone 13 on WebKit and 1280 x 800 on Chromium, served by Vite on 5201), B ran the detector and the lint. No decision page, live mode or question tool was opened (owner instruction 2026-10-07); "Questions skipped: the run is unattended and the owner told every session to proceed".

Heuristics (A): 28 of 40, up from 25 at V1. Strongest: the settings line (a timetable row, mono figures, no accent), links with history, loading inside the tab. Weakest: consistency (2) and aesthetic and minimalist design (2).

Design specificity (A): the settings line is the first piece of shell chrome that could only be Carta's; everything around it is still generic kit, and the chrome shouts louder than the instrument.

Priority issues:

1. [P1] The accent is the loudest thing on every shell screen. On the phone the pass chip is an accent-tinted pill with an accent border, the wordmark is accent, the active nav item is accent text with an accent tick, and the plus wears an accent ring on a planner tab; on desktop the avatar is an accent disc and the active tab has an accent underline (24-destination-workspace.css overrides 10-shell.css's ink pill). 2. [P1] The active section is drawn two ways, accent on the phone and an accent underline on desktop, against the selected-state rule (ink fill, both widths). (quieter, then animate)
3. [P1] The Lifestyle dialog is outside the dialog family and is no instrument: two accent-filled tiles, a letter "x" on a grey disc half off its corner, the bottom nav drawn over its last row on a phone, no trade-off sentence and no figure. (colorize to remove, then polish)
4. [P2] Nothing in the shell answers with motion from the tokens: the chooser rises with a literal 180 ms, the sheets with literal curves, the price pill overshoots (detector `bounce-easing`, 14-filters-panels.css 267), four `transition: all` remain, and a tab change is a hard cut. (animate)
5. [P2] Three scrims (ink 28 percent, ink 44 percent, a black 36 and 42 percent with blur), three closes (CloseIcon, letter "x", the grey disc), dialog radii of 12, 14, 18 and 20 px, and sheet shadows that are literals. (polish)

Detector (B): the JSX files are clean (exit 0); the CSS finds two warnings in 14-filters-panels.css, `bounce-easing` at 267 and `side-tab` (an accent stripe) at 2659. Design lint: 194 found, 194 baselined, 0 new; the shell's baseline entries are 5 in 10-shell.css (4 shadow literals, 1 outline-none) and 13 in 14-filters-panels.css (12 shadow literals, 1 outline-none).

Where A and B agree: the overshoot curve. What B caught alone: the accent side stripe (2659). What A caught alone: everything about the accent on the chrome, which no detector rule reads.

## Motion thesis (impeccable animate)

Focal moment: none authored; the shell is Operate and must not perform. Continuity: a tab change (new content fades and rises 8 px, header and bar stay still, the active pill slides between items), sheets and dialogs arriving and leaving, the Lifestyle sheet rising over the bar. Feedback: press scale on nav items and the plus, the selected tile filling in ink at `--dur-1`, a figure that dips once when a setting changes it. Budget: transform and opacity only, `--dur-1` to `--dur-3`, nothing on scroll, nothing that loops; the View Transition for a tab change runs only when the API exists and reduced motion is off.

## Changes in order

1. The chrome recedes (brief V4-1, rules R4 and the accent-marks rule). Bottom nav on `--paper` with a `--rule` top border, no blur and no shadow. The plus sits inside the bar (no 26 px rise, so it no longer covers whatever sits above the bar: rows T381-g and T382-c, shell half), stays a secondary round button, and on a planner tab is `--ink-fill` with an `--on-fill` glyph instead of the accent ring. The active section is an `--ink-fill` pill on both widths: behind the icon on the phone (label in ink, weight 600), behind the label on desktop (replacing the accent underline). The phone pass chip takes the desktop secondary (transparent, `--rule`, 6 px, `--tap`; the accent stays on its glyph only, the T360 owner call that verify_top_bar asserts). The avatar becomes a secondary round button (`--bg-card`, `--rule`, ink initial). The phone wordmark is ink, and the brand's hover no longer turns it accent. The Lifestyle door loses its accent tint and accent glyph (DIRECTION.md part 9, "no tint on the Lifestyle door"). The chooser's icons go to `--ink-soft`. Files: `src/styles/60-v6-shell.css` (overrides of 24-destination-workspace.css and 16-auth.css, which other surfaces own), `src/styles/10-shell.css` (the bar, the plus and the chooser, edited in place). Check: no accent fill or accent text in the header or the bar in any screenshot except the pass chip's glyph.

2. Motion from the tokens (brief V4-2). The plan chooser, the filter and shell sheets (`.fsheet`), the Lifestyle panel, the settings popovers, the bottom slot's messages and the nav press take `--dur-1` to `--dur-3` with `--ease-out` and `--ease-in-out`; exits in `--dur-2`. Exits need the element to stay mounted while it leaves: a small `usePresence(open)` hook in `src/lib/motion.js` keeps the chooser and the Lifestyle panel for `--dur-2` with a leaving class (0 under reduced motion). In 14-filters-panels.css: the price pill's overshoot becomes `--ease-out` at `--dur-1` (detector `bounce-easing` gone), the four `transition: all` name their properties, and the sheet rules read the tokens. Every new rule carries its reduced-motion branch. Files: `src/lib/motion.js`, `src/components/BottomNav.jsx`, `src/App.jsx`, `src/styles/10-shell.css`, `src/styles/14-filters-panels.css`, `src/styles/60-v6-shell.css`. Check: the detector reports no bounce; no shell rule written in this wave names a literal duration.

3. A section change that reads as movement (brief V4-3, rule R5). `goToTab` runs the state change inside `document.startViewTransition` when the API exists, reduced motion is off, the tab really changes and no other transition (the shared photograph) is running. The header row and the bottom nav carry their own `view-transition-name` with no animation, so they never move; the rest of the page (the new tab) fades in and rises 8 px in `--dur-2` `--ease-out`, and the outgoing tab does not animate. A transform on the tab wrapper was rejected: every tab root is `position: fixed` inside a `display: contents` shell, and a transformed ancestor would collapse them. The active pill slides between items: one marker per nav, placed from the measured active item through two custom properties, moving by `transform` in `--dur-2` `--ease-in-out`; it jumps under reduced motion. Files: `src/App.jsx`, `src/components/AppHeader.jsx`, `src/components/BottomNav.jsx`, `src/styles/60-v6-shell.css`. Check: the transition is 200 ms and absent under reduced motion; verify_tab_switching and verify_keyboard still pass.

4. Figures that update in place (brief V4-4). The settings line's own figures (dates, party, Lifestyle's bed and vibe) settle through `useSettledFigure()` from `src/lib/figureChange.js` when they change, and so does the Lifestyle trade-off figure. The shell's trigger for every other surface is that same hook: a surface wraps its figure in a ref and passes the figure's value, so only a figure that changed dips. Files: `src/components/SettingsLine.jsx`, `src/browse/LifestylePanel.jsx`. Check: changing party size dips the party value once; no box changes width.

5. One dialog family (brief V4-5, rules R7 and R10). One scrim, `--ink` at 28 percent through `color-mix`, no blur, on the Lifestyle panel, the plan chooser, the share-trip offer, the three legal modals and the filter sheets. One close, `.shell-close`: CloseIcon 16 px in a `--tap` box, transparent, `--ink-soft`, translated "Close", on the Lifestyle panel, the share-trip offer and the legal modals (the sheet close already is CloseIcon and takes the same look). Dialogs 12 px, sheets 12 px on their top corners, popovers 10 px; `--shadow-2` only. The share-trip offer gains `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal` and its title as label. Files: `src/App.jsx`, `src/browse/LifestylePanel.jsx`, `src/components/PrivacyPolicy.jsx`, `TermsOfService.jsx`, `Imprint.jsx`, `src/styles/14-filters-panels.css`, `src/styles/60-v6-shell.css`. Check: one scrim value and one close in every dialog the shell draws.

6. Lifestyle as a priced instrument (brief V4-6, rule R8). Selected tiles and hotel grades in `--ink-fill`, the grade row on `--paper-dim` instead of the accent tint. A footer under the choices states the trade-off with a computed figure: the next cheaper way to sleep (or the next dearer one when the traveller is already on the cheapest), in the shape "A private room instead: €N less a night per person in <town>" for the destination page that is open, and as the median of that difference over every place where both tiers were measured ("..., the median of N places") when none is. The figure is `computeCosts()` from `src/lib/costIndex.js` at the two tiers, set with `eurDay()`, the formatter the cards use, so it agrees with them; no sentence when the data cannot support one. One primary, "Done", closes the dialog. On a phone the panel is a bottom sheet over the bar (above the nav in z-order, over the one scrim), with a grab handle whose swipe is a shortcut for the close and Done. Files: `src/browse/LifestylePanel.jsx`, `src/App.jsx` (passes the open destination), `src/styles/14-filters-panels.css`, `src/styles/60-v6-shell.css`, six catalogues (`lifestyle.*`).
