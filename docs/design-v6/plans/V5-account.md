## Audit and critique of the V4 result (impeccable audit, then critique, unattended)

Method. One context, not the dual-agent split of V3 and V4: the V4 result is already critiqued in `V4-account.md` and the open questions are measured rather than judged, so the audit is a measurement. Setup ran from the root worktree (`impeccable context --target src/auth/SavedTripsPanel.jsx` loaded PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md). Questions skipped: the run is unattended (owner instruction 2026-10-07). Measured with Playwright scripts kept outside both repos (`wt/_scratch/T410`) on the merged V5 state, on iPhone 13 (WebKit), Pixel 5, 360x740 and desktop 1280x800, against the Vite dev server on port 5210.

Audit.

1. Accessibility and touch. The touch census (controls under 44 px on a coarse pointer) finds, outside My trips: the account spokes' back button (82x30), Friends copy and share (59x32, 123x32) and the "about" dot (24x24), the password reveal (40x40) and the "forgot" link (146x23), the feedback kind pills (36 high), the sign-in submit and fields (43 high), the guide country chips (30 high), the guide back and copy buttons (27 and 34 high). On My trips Visited: the record map's expand control (30x30), "Add a past trip" (123x27) and the crew "+" (28x28, three of them). On desktop the rail rows and a few buttons are under 44, which a fine pointer allows (32 px). The shared trip view draws no h1 at all. Errors: six paths still print a raw `err.message`.
2. Performance. No metric on this surface is promised; images are lazy with a srcSet. The one page-wide cost I can name is the 87 hover rules outside `(hover: hover)` which cost nothing to load but stick after a tap.
3. Theming. Two `side-tab` stripes in `22-account-admin.css` (the admin warning at line 794 and the site banner at 1858). 24 literal transition durations in 16-auth and 22-account-admin (6 and 18), one literal animation (`confirm-toast-in .25s`), one filter transition in 17. The design lint finds 149 violations, 147 in the baseline and 2 new that are not this surface's (`src/lib/destinationPdf.js:40` and `:41`, hex literals).
4. Responsive. No sideways scroll on any walked view at 360, 393 or 390 px. On an iPhone 13 with the install hint up, the focal card's actions end at 671 px of a 664 px screen after the shell's V5 change (T400 measured 648).
5. Anti-patterns. The detector finds the two side-tabs above and nothing in the JSX.

Critique. The V4 build did the thing the brief asked for on My trips: nothing sits on a photograph, the next trip is the focal card, removal answers with Undo. What is left is the edge of the surface rather than its middle. The account spokes, the sign-in modal, the guides and the shared trip were left at V3 sizes: they read as Carta but ask a thumb to hit 24 to 30 px. The hover and motion audit was explicitly left for V5. The admin page still carries two accent stripes and a green flag switch. No finding contradicts the palette or the type.

## The changes, in order

1. Touch targets (brief V5-1, impeccable adapt). Every control listed in the audit reaches `--tap` on a coarse pointer, written in `69-v6-account.css` under `@media (pointer: coarse)`: account back, Friends copy, share and about, password reveal, auth links, sign-in submit and fields, feedback kind pills, guide chips, back and copy, the record map's expand, add a past trip, the crew plus, the install hint's buttons. Desktop (fine pointer) keeps its 32 px rows. Check: the census reads 0 under 44 on the three phones for every view of this surface, except the map library's own attribution (not this surface's).
2. The focal card inside the first screen again (brief V5-6, adapt). On a phone the focal photograph is 2:1 and shrinks to 5:2 when the screen is shorter than 700 px of layout height, so the actions end inside the screen on an iPhone 13 with the install hint up. Check: `actionsBottom <= vh` on the three phones.
3. Motion and hover (brief V5-3, impeccable animate audit side). Every transition and animation in 16, 22 and 17 reads `--dur-1` or `--dur-2` and `--ease-out`; no decoration animates `filter` or `box-shadow`; every `:hover` rule in 16, 17, 22, 40, 42 and 69 sits inside `@media (hover: hover)` (a mechanical wrap of the selectors that carry `:hover`, leaving the others where they are); each file that has transitions or animations carries a reduced-motion branch. 21-chat-passes.css is the pass half and the chat half in one file: the chat half is the day planner's, so its 17 hover rules stay (register row). Check: the tally script reads 0 literal durations and 0 ungated hover rules in the five files; verify_quality_floor.
4. Focus and dialogs (brief V5-4, impeccable harden). The full record map gets `aria-modal`, `role="dialog"` and `useFocusTrap`; the shared trip view closes on Escape and carries one h1; card menus take arrow keys and focus their first item; the confirm rows that remain (publish, withdraw a link) move focus to the safe button and Escape keeps; after a removal by keyboard focus lands on the toast's Undo (row T400-d); the crew name's remove goes through the undo hold and the house close glyph (row T400-e); the admin content editor gets `aria-modal`, its own Escape and a trap. Check: verify_keyboard 20 of 20, a scripted Tab walk of the sign-in modal and the pass modal, the harnesses.
5. Language (brief V5-5, impeccable clarify and harden). The raw `err.message` paths and the two-sentence generic errors go through one-sentence i18n keys; dates on this surface follow the traveller's language, not `en-GB`; the hard-coded "to" and "day(s)" in the shared view, the friend trip panel and My trips become keys. Six catalogues, parsed before the commit. Check: a German walk of My trips and the shared view shows no English word from this surface's own strings.
6. Widths and long content (brief V5-6). 360, 380 and 390 px with no sideways scroll on every view; a 60-character trip name, a 40-character person name and a trip with no cities and no dates are drawn without overflow. Check: the walk's hscroll is 0 and the long-name probe wraps.
7. Admin consistency (brief V5-8, impeccable polish). The two accent stripes go (a status callout becomes a bordered box with an amber dot, per the shell's amber tokens); the selected admin tab is `--ink-fill`; count badges are neutral; the feedback kind chips leave `--danger` and `--green` for the ink ramp; the flag switch leaves green; revert and password-reset email get an inline confirm. Sparkbars print the peak and the last value. What an admin change needs beyond this (the Site tab's one primary) is written as a row if the code does not allow it in a small edit. Check: verify_admin_panel twice back to back.
8. The seven carta-design questions and the detector (brief V5-9). `impeccable detect --json` over every changed file, 0 findings; the design lint 0 new from this surface.
9. Harness updates (brief V5-7). Every assertion that encoded the old design is updated with a comment saying why. The eight harnesses, verify_keyboard, verify_quality_floor and the scripted phone walk run at the end.
