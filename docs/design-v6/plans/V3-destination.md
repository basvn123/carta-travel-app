# V3 plan: Destination (town) page

T385, design wave V3, 2026-10-08. Surface key `destination`. Written in planning mode before the first app edit, from `docs/design-v6/briefs/destination.md` (part 6, V3 list, and its Direction check), `docs/design-v6/DIRECTION.md`, `docs/design-v6/OWNERSHIP.md` and the carta-design skill. This plan is committed first and then carried out; the report (`Execution/P10/T385-v6-v3-destination.md`) says what was done and what stayed open.

## Shape (impeccable, run unattended)

`impeccable context` ran from the root worktree and loaded PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md. The owner's instruction of 2026-10-07 tells this run to proceed without asking, so shape's interview was not opened; the brief's part 5 and 6 are the answers it would have collected, and every open choice below takes the option that keeps Carta's palette, type and identity. Refinement, not redesign.

Job and audience: a budget traveller, often a hiker, often on a phone, mid-comparison, deciding on one town. Outcome: they learn what a day costs, whether their month is good, whether they can get there and around without a car, and what there is to walk and ride, in that order, and leave by setting dates, saving the place or opening a planner. Direction: an instrument page; the receipt is the focal object, the head is one row of three actions, everything else is a fold in the traveller's order. Boundaries: no token, no new colour, no visual pass (V4), no tap-target or overflow pass (V5); only files OWNERSHIP.md gives to `destination` plus `src/styles/64-v6-destination.css` and the six i18n catalogues under `dest.*`.

## Constraints that change the brief's V3 list

1. T374-d (two day-cost bases) is still the owner's decision. This wave does not pick a basis. The fact strip's "A day here" (cost index at the Lifestyle) and the first-run receipt (its own stay tier) both stay, as they are, and each says its basis in words. The cost fold stays as the Lifestyle-basis view, moves up beside the receipt, and loses only its rounded trip-total line and its duplicate price report button.
2. `FirstRunReceipt.jsx` is landing's, `HonestFooters.jsx` destinations', `SectionRail.jsx`, `Fold.jsx`, `MonthStrip.jsx` and `HeroImage.jsx` shell's, `CostSummary.jsx` explore's. They are used, never edited. What V3 needs from them is a register row.
3. T380-e: the settings line (T381) is not in this branch. The page reads the traveller's month from the same shared dates the settings line will set (`useFirstRun()`: the receipt's arrive date). The page has no month select of its own today, so there is none to keep; the receipt's Arrive field is the month input until the settings line lands.

## Changes, in order

1. Order by the traveller's questions (brief V3-2). New DOM order, which is the phone order: head, fact strip, receipt, cost at your Lifestyle, month block, getting there, walk and ride from here, things to do, highlights and map, trips, villages inside, rating, where to sleep, tips, festivals, parking, webcams, what to bring, explore further, the page-end utility row, the honest footer. From 1020 px the side column holds receipt, cost, month and getting there at its top, the main column starts at walk and ride, the remaining side folds follow under getting there. Files: `src/browse/DestinationPage.jsx`, `src/styles/64-v6-destination.css`.
2. One action row (V3-3). Head actions: Plan a trip here (primary once the dates are the traveller's), Plan a day here, Shortlist. The head Share goes (the bar's Share stays). Download PDF guide and Report a problem move to one row of secondary buttons before the honest footer. Open in Google Maps moves into the map fold. Files: DestinationPage.jsx, 64-v6-destination.css.
3. Fact strip (V3-1 within T374-d, V3-3). Sights merges into the visit-length line ("Worth about 2 days, about 2 h of sights"); Best months moves into the month block; the strip keeps "A day here at your Lifestyle" and "Beds from", each labelled with its basis. File: DestinationPage.jsx, i18n.
4. One month block (V3-4). When to go, Weather this week, the crowd badge and the water badge become one fold whose closed summary answers ("best Apr, May; quiet"). ClimateStrip (this surface's) shows every month's high, low and rain as text without hover, and outlines the traveller's month 2 px `--ink` (Month rule). MonthStrip stays shell's; adopting it is a hand-off row. Files: DestinationPage.jsx, src/browse/ClimateStrip.jsx, 64-v6-destination.css.
5. Getting there leads car-free (V3-5): rows in the order transit verdict, airport transfer, car verdict; the closed summary answers the car-free question first. File: src/browse/GettingThere.jsx, DestinationPage.jsx.
6. Walk and ride from here (V3-6): Routes from here and Around become one fold (`#sec-routes`), routes first, the 20 km inventory under it, one empty line where there is neither. Files: DestinationPage.jsx, src/browse/AroundHere.jsx (singular plurals).
7. Section rail (V3-7, T165-b): `.destp-subnav` is replaced by the shared SectionRail (anchors, ink-fill pill, appears once the head has scrolled away). Expand all moves to the page bar (SectionRail has no trailing slot; register row to shell), keeps its class `destp-subnav-all` for the harnesses, includes the empty folds and keeps acting after the dossier settles.
8. Remembered folds (V3-8, Remembered state rule): open folds per destination in sessionStorage behind try/catch, restored on a return within the session, defaults for a new destination.
9. Copy (V3-9): titles with a verb or a number ("See the highlights on the map", "Read the insider tips", "N festivals and events", "N villages and towns inside", "Walk and ride from here"), singulars in the Around summary, the pack summary without its colon; six catalogues, an owner row for native review. Keys only under `dest.*`.
10. Airport out of the place (V3-10): `baseCity()` on the receipt's place (passed in, FirstRunReceipt unchanged) and on a gallery caption that is the record's own name.
11. What to bring packs for the traveller's month (the receipt's arrive month) when there is one, else the next best month as today.
