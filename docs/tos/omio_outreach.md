Hello Omio partnerships team,

I run Carta (carta-europetravel.com), a price map for budget travel in Europe.
It prices the whole trip, flights, stays and ground legs, across more than
24,000 European destinations. Carta is a live Omio affiliate through the
Impact network: every train, bus and ferry leg in the planner already links
out to the matching Omio journey page through our tracking link. The prices we
show next to those links today are our own estimates, clearly labelled as
estimates.

I have one question. May we cache Omio prices and display them on our
discovery map, with each price labelled with the time we retrieved it and
clicking through to the matching Omio journey page via our Impact tracking
link?

To make the ask concrete:

- Display: a price on a map pin or route line, marked with its freshness, for
  example "checked 3 hours ago".
- Caching: short lived, hours rather than weeks, so the map stays responsive
  without hammering your systems.
- Booking: every displayed price clicks through to Omio for the live fare and
  the actual purchase, so Omio remains the point of sale.

If this is better served by your B2B search API, or if there are display
rules or a maximum cache age you would want us to follow, I will gladly work
within those.
