Hello Flix distribution team,

I run Carta (carta-europetravel.com), a price map for budget travel in
Europe. It prices the whole trip, flights, stays and ground legs, across more
than 24,000 European destinations. For coach legs Carta currently shows its
own estimates, clearly labelled as estimates, and sends travellers to the
operator to book. We also read the public Flix GTFS feed for the route
network itself: schedules and stops only, no prices.

I have one question. May we cache FlixBus prices and display them on our
discovery map, with each price labelled with the time we retrieved it and
clicking through to flixbus.com to book?

To make the ask concrete:

- Display: a price on a route line or map pin, marked with its freshness, for
  example "checked 3 hours ago".
- Caching: short lived, hours rather than weeks, so the map stays responsive
  without putting load on your systems.
- Booking: every displayed price clicks through to flixbus.com for the live
  fare and the actual purchase, so Flix remains the point of sale.

If this runs through a specific partner programme or API on your side, point
me at it and we will follow that route, including any display rules or cache
age limits you set.

Thank you for your time.
