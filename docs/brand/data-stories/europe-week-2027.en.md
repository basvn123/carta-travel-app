# Draft: A week in Europe costs less than a week at home in Belgium

Status: draft for the owner (T1130, ROADMAP K5). Not published. Every figure is from `data/basket_2027.json` (run of the shipped data, bed prices measured 23 to 30 October 2026); see [method.md](method.md). Rerun the script on publication day.

Audience: Belgian and Dutch press, English edition for the blog and international pick-up.

---

**A week of bed, food and drink costs one person a median of EUR 1,093 in Belgium. In 26 of the 33 European countries Carta ranks, the same week is cheaper.**

Carta, a Europe trip planner, priced the same week at 3,353 places across Europe: an apartment shared by two, dinners and lunches out, a coffee a day, a few beers and one night out. Same basket everywhere; bed prices measured in October 2026, food prices as of September 2026.

The European median for that week is EUR 910 a person. Belgium sits at EUR 1,093, 27th of 33. Only Austria (EUR 1,132), the Netherlands (EUR 1,194), Norway (EUR 1,209), the United Kingdom (EUR 1,325), Ireland (EUR 1,380) and Switzerland (EUR 1,659) come out dearer.

The surprise for a Belgian traveller is how close the big holiday countries are. France, Italy and Spain are the usual summer picks, and a week there saves EUR 159 (Spain) to EUR 247 (France) a person against the same week in Belgium. The real gap opens in Central and Eastern Europe: in Poland the week costs EUR 515 less, in Czechia EUR 483 less.

## The bed is the bill

In Belgium the bed is 60% of the day: a median EUR 94.37 a person a night for half an apartment, against EUR 61.63 for food and drink. In Portugal the bed share is 69%, in Spain 67%: the meals are cheap, the apartment is where the money goes. Anyone hunting a cheaper week should look at the bed before the restaurant.

## Belgium itself: from EUR 755 in Charleroi to EUR 1,305 in Bruges

Within Belgium, the same week runs from EUR 755 in Charleroi and EUR 824 in Liège to EUR 1,305 in Bruges. Ghent (EUR 972) costs less than Brussels (EUR 1,085) and Antwerp (EUR 1,087). On the coast, Ostend comes to EUR 1,169 and De Haan to EUR 1,296. A week in Charleroi costs less than the median week in Italy.

## The Netherlands: dearer than Belgium

A Dutch week comes to EUR 1,194, 29th of 33 and 9% dearer than Belgium. Amsterdam is EUR 1,359, Utrecht EUR 1,395, while Rotterdam (EUR 1,002) and The Hague (EUR 1,050) stay below the Belgian median.

## How we counted

One person, seven nights, two people sharing an entire apartment, eating out most days. Bed prices are Carta's own measurements on Booking.com (October 2026), with an estimate of cleaning and service fees. Food and drink prices are modelled from measured prices in 24 countries and Eurostat price levels; they are good for comparing countries, not for the exact cent. The flight or train there and local transport are not in the basket. Countries with fewer than 10 priced places (Luxembourg, Malta, Iceland and seven more) are left out of the ranking. Full method: [method.md](method.md).
