# Notice of the Terms change of 12 November 2026 (T333-f)

The Terms promise account holders 30 days' notice by email before a change takes effect ("Availability and changes"). The changes of 12 November 2026 (photos and tracks you share, the withdrawal right, passes that never renew) are such a change. This is the email; it is a one-off legal notice, not marketing, so it needs no consent.

**Who sends it:** a session with Supabase and Resend access, once Resend sends from carta-europetravel.com (T213-e). Recipients: every confirmed account in `auth.users`. Record the send date in `docs/owner/legal.md`. Uploads go live no earlier than the send date plus 30 days (`docs/owner/uploads.md`).

**Subject:** Carta's terms change on [SEND DATE + 30 DAYS]

**Body:**

> Hello,
>
> We are updating Carta's terms of service. The new terms apply from [SEND DATE + 30 DAYS], 30 days after this email.
>
> What changes:
> 1. Passes have not been on sale yet; the new terms describe them: a Trip Pass and a Year Pass that both end on their own and never renew, and your right to withdraw within 14 days.
> 2. Photos and tracks you share: a new section sets out the licence you give Carta when you send a photo or GPS track, how to have one removed, and the separate box that lets a track go to OpenStreetMap.
>
> Read the new terms: https://carta-europetravel.com/?legal=terms
>
> If you do not agree, you can delete your account at any time from the Account panel; nothing is charged.
>
> Questions: hello@carta-europetravel.com
>
> Carta
