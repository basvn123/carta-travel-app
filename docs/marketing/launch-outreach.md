## Text 1: courtesy note to a credited body (send on Tue 4 May 2027, after the launch posts are up)

One note per body, filled by hand, never a mail merge.

Subject: Carta credits {{BODY}}

Hello,

I am {{OWNER_NAME}}, and I build Carta, one app for planning a trip anywhere in Europe: it plans the whole trip in one place, with what each day costs. Carta launched on Tuesday 4 May 2027, after a public beta from 9 March. I am writing to say that it uses {{WHAT_WE_SHOW}} from {{BODY}}, under {{LICENCE}}, and that we credit it in the app.

The credit sits with the other sources on one page that needs no account: {{CREDIT_URL}}. If the wording is wrong, or you would like it changed, reply and I will fix it the same week.

Nothing is needed from you. This is a courtesy, not a request, and it does not claim that you endorse Carta. Please tell me if anything we show from your data looks wrong.

Thank you for publishing it.

{{OWNER_NAME}}

Rules for the note. Fill {{WHAT_WE_SHOW}} from the `credit` line of that body's entry in attribution.js, shortened to one clause. Never write "open data", "partner" or "sponsor". Never ask the body to share, post or link.

Who gets one (T336-b, decided 2026-11-12): every credited outside body, including CARTO, OpenTripMap, Exchange Rate API and MET Norway; not the entry for Carta's own image copies; the EEA gets one note for both its entries; UNESCO only after a session has read the World Heritage Centre terms of use and recorded the licence (ROADMAP I3).
