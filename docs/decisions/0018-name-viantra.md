# 0018 · The product is renamed Viantra

## Context
The app has been called Carta since the start, live at carta-europetravel.com. On 2026-11-12 the owner decided to keep the name (T1060-a, ROADMAP K1). On 2026-11-26 the owner reopened it as the first step of a brand redesign (name, logo, colours, style, a new `BRAND.md`): the new name had to be unique and feel like a travel brand.

What weighed against Carta (`docs/brand/POSITIONING.md`): Carta, Inc. holds US CARTA marks in classes 9 and 42, @carta is taken on X and TikTok, CARTA is a US transit agency's trip planner, and the domain is long.

## Options considered
All checks were quick and read-only on 2026-11-26: TMview (EU, Benelux, main EU offices, UK, CH, WIPO; classes 9, 35, 39, 42, 43; filed and registered), registry RDAP or DNS for domains, a web search, and a look at the app stores and social profiles.
1. **Etapa** (a stage of a journey). Rejected: descriptive in Spanish, Portuguese, Romanian, Czech and Slovak; close travel marks (VALENCIA VIAJES ETAPA, ES, class 39; ETAPAS, FR, classes 39 and 43; ETAP, EUIPO, 35 and 39); etapa.com is a Brazilian school group, etapa.eu is for sale at 3,000 EUR.
2. **About 100 coined, suggestive, compound, Latin, Italian and English names.** Most short `.com` domains were registered. Cleanest at the time: Paseda, Saunta, Zwervo (the only free `.com`), Holiva, Zigsteps, Driftbird, Girovago, Peragro. TMview blocked the session partway through the last round, so those last names are unchecked in the register.
3. **Viantra**, from *via* (the way) and *tra* (Italian: between, through), "the way through". Chosen.

## Decision
The product's name is **Viantra**. Pronounced vee-AN-tra.

Facts found for it on 2026-11-26:
- **Register:** no exact Viantra mark. Nearest: **Viantro** (EUIPO 018063042 and 018141650, DPMA, UKIPO; classes 35, 36, 41, 42; Doctari's job platform for doctors), **Vintra** (AT, classes 35 and 39), **Viastra** (FR, 9, 16, 41, 42, 44, 45).
- **Same name in use:** a small Spanish-language travel agency "Viantra Travel" on TikTok (@viantra.travel, 12 followers on 2026-11-26, no website found, no mark found).
- **Domains:** free at the registries: viantra.travel, viantra.app, viantra.voyage, viantra.tours, getviantra.com, viantratravel.com, goviantra.com, viantraapp.com, viantrahq.com. Taken: viantra.com (GoDaddy since 2014, owner hidden, not listed on HugeDomains), viantra.eu.
- **Handles:** @viantra is taken on Instagram, TikTok, X, Pinterest and GitHub (mostly empty accounts). @viantratravel and @getviantra looked free on Instagram, TikTok and X.

The owner accepted the two known risks (Viantro's likeness in class 42, and the Viantra Travel agency) knowingly.

## Consequences
- This supersedes T1060-a (keep Carta) in ROADMAP K1. `PRODUCT.md` is name-free and does not change for the name itself.
- Before any brand spend: a professional clearance search that covers Viantro and Viantra Travel, then a filing in class 39 and class 9 (class 42 only on that search's advice). Owner steps in `docs/owner/accounts-and-signups.md`.
- The rename in the code is its own later piece of work: about 440 app source files, the six catalogues, "Vraag Carta" (Ask's name), the manifest, the store icons, the share cards, the domain and redirects, the e-mail domain. The `carta.*` keys in browser storage keep their names, so no traveller loses a saved trip.
- The brand redesign (logo, colours, style, `BRAND.md`) builds on this name.
- Undo: write a new decision; nothing in the code has changed yet.

## How we'll know it works
The clearance search comes back without a blocking conflict, the chosen domain and handles are registered in the owner's name, and the class 39 filing is accepted.
