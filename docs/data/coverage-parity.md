## Method

**Fair expectation per country.** A country's fair share of the catalogue is a weighted mean of three shares: its share of residents, of land area and of tourist nights across the catalogue's countries, weighted population 0.25, area 0.25 and nights 0.5 (nights measure travel demand directly; population and area keep a large country with few visitors in view). Expected places = catalogue size times that share. Population and area come from the reference table `COUNTRY_AREA_POP` in `pipeline/diagnostics/coverage_report.py` (World Bank and national statistics, mid-2020s, imported, never copied). Tourist nights are Eurostat `tour_occ_nin3` (nights spent at tourist accommodation, NUTS 3, latest year per region, 2022 to 2025), read from the tracked `cache/eurostat_nights_nuts3.json`; licence: Eurostat reuse policy, CC BY 4.0. Eurostat holds no nights for AD, BA, FO, GB, MC, MD, SM, XK; for those the nights share is the mean of their population and area shares, so they are judged on two terms, not penalised for a missing third.

- A country under 0.67 of its expectation is a place gap (D15). The gap is also tested under three other blends (equal thirds; population and nights half each; nights alone) and marked robust when it holds under all of them.
- Regions are NUTS 2. A place (at its city point where it has one) takes the NUTS 3 code its crowding block carries when that code is in its own country, else the GISCO NUTS 2021 polygon it falls in (tracked `cache/nuts3_2021.geojson`, EuroGeographics notice; the 2021 set still holds the UK codes, whose suffixes match ITL 2), else the nearest NUTS 3 centre in its country (`nearest`). A country with no NUTS polygon but region pages takes the region of the nearest located item in `public/region/` (`itl_nearest`, Kosovo); one with neither counts as one region (`country`). The French outermost regions (FRY) are outside the catalogue's Europe and left out. Assignment: country 41, crowding 3,268, itl_nearest 8, nearest 57, polygon 494.
- A region's expectation is its country's places times the mean of its share of the country's area and nights (area alone where nights are missing, an equal split where both are missing). A region with an expectation of 2 or more that holds under 50% of it is a region gap (D15). A city region (under 2,000 km2, such as Praha or Wien) is one city and so one place, however many nights it draws: its expectation is capped at 2. Only regions with a nights figure are tested: area alone would ask for dozens of places in empty uplands. The United Kingdom, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, Moldova and the microstates are listed but not tested until regional nights exist for them (D11).
- A field is a gap in a country when its share sits 15 points or more under the catalogue share and 3 or more places are short.
- Outdoor: `public/coverage.json` gives each coverage unit (NUTS 3, coast or range) a status per layer and marks a layer `na` where it does not apply (beaches include lake bathing spots, so an inland unit can be `empty`). A country whose share of empty units (`na` excluded) is 15 points over the catalogue rate is a gap; so is a country with 50 or more rated trails where under 25% carry a photo.
- Languages: the why line per language from `public/app_data/why_i18n/`; the intro from each place's dossier, shown in English only (handbook, Discover).
- Ranking: place gaps first (they feed D15), then fields, outdoor and language; inside each kind, by the number of places (or regions, or items) short.
- Not measured here: Ask recall per language needs the E1 question set (register row T1145-c). Field shares are what the wire holds, not what is true (D4 checks truth).
