## Rules

- Add: the scorer and gates of `score_place_candidates.py` and `promote_place_candidates.py` (worth 0.4 or more, not covered, 6 km apart, anchor within 400 km), then the D2 check on the spec's core fields.
- Merge: same QID or GeoNames id; same folded name or a GeoNames alternate name within 10 km (25 km for 300,000 people or more). Review: names 0.90 alike within 5 km.
- Remove or fix: D2 fields with a rule floor of 0.90 or more, present and valid against the wire schema; an identity field means remove.
