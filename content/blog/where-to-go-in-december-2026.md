## How to read it

High C is the usual daytime high in December and Rain mm the usual rain for the month, both from NASA POWER normals for 2001 to 2020. Crowds is the month's tier from Eurostat and JRC overnight stays per square kilometre. Rating is Carta's rating out of 10. Day EUR is an indicative day for one person: half a mid-range hotel room, one cheap meal and one mid-range meal, one person.

## What the numbers say

The lowest day on the list is Merida at 65 EUR, and the highest is Bonifacio at 100 EUR. These are estimates from Carta's price model, not quotes: open a place to see the sources behind its bed and food figures.

## How we chose them

A place is listed when its weather comfort in December is in the highest tenth of all places, its crowd tier for December is quiet or moderate, and its Carta rating is 7.0 or more. The list is ranked by the indicative day, lowest first, with at most 2 places a country. The data is Carta's catalogue as of 2026-06-07.
