# The finish, and the record of choices (2026-11-26)

## No designer
The owner chose on 2026-11-26 not to hire a designer. Step 5 is Claude's finish, signed off by the owner.

What that means:
- **Quality:** the mark is built from exact arcs and straight lines, where code-drawn SVG is clean; the curve problems in the research came from hand-drawn shapes. Letter spacing is set by eye, pair by pair, at 1600 px wide.
- **Trademark:** unchanged. Registration rests on distinctiveness, not on who drew the logo.
- **Copyright:** weaker without a human drawing. The owner's own creative choices below are the best evidence of human authorship; keep this file with the logo master.
