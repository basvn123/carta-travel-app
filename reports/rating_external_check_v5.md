# External check of the v5 ratings (T760)

Our top 200 agrees with the outside references only weakly, mostly because the two published lists name few places in our catalogue: 28 in all, 10 of them in our top 200. Of the 30 largest gaps, 21 hold on review and 9 need a different appeal, proposed below for the owner. Nothing here is applied. This closes the review step of T469-d once the owner has read it.

Measured on the v5.0-r2 ratings of 3,855 places (batch R2, T757 to T760), 2026-10-27.

## Rank correlation over our top 200

Spearman correlation of our score with each reference signal, over our 200 highest-rated places. A place a reference does not name counts as 0.

| Tripadvisor Travelers' Choice | +0.034 | 6 | points by rank in each list, summed over lists and years |

| UNESCO World Heritage at the place | +0.159 | 106 | number of World Heritage registers at the place |
| Wikivoyage guide | +0.184 | 128 | 1 when the place has a guide (star status is not harvested) |

All four correlations are positive and small. The two lists are too thin to say much: Tripadvisor published no 2026 Europe list (only world and theme lists), and Lonely Planet dropped its ranked city and region lists from 2026 on (25 destinations plus 25 experiences, unranked). Places the lists name that the catalogue has no row for: none; Sicily, Istanbul, Tbilisi and most regions have no row, so they could not be compared.

## The 30 largest gaps

Two kinds, 15 each. Named, scored low: a published list names the place and we rank it far down. Top, unsupported: we score it 9.0 or more and no list, World Heritage register or Wikivoyage guide supports it. Each verdict was made from the pages linked in its row, opened during the review. Appeal is the hand scale (before the class curve), the number a correction would change.

## What the review found beyond the scores

- Proposed adjustments: 9, listed in the tables above (Belgrade 6.0 to 6.3, Toulouse 6.5 to 6.8, Marseille 6.5 to 6.8, Castel Gandolfo 7.1 to 6.8, Ortisei (Val Gardena) 8.6 to 8.0, Reykjavik (Keflavik) 8.0 to 7.3, Tenno 7.2 to 6.8, Cala Gonone (Golfo di Orosei) 8.0 to 7.4, Fort William (Glencoe) 8.2 to 7.2). Five of them lower a village or town whose appeal was credited with its surrounding landscape (Ortisei, Cala Gonone, Fort William, Reykjavik, Tenno).
- Airport rows standing for a city: MXP "Milan (Malpensa)" and KEF "Reykjavik (Keflavik)" sit at the airport, about 50 km out, so their beauty and depth are measured there.
- Classes that look wrong: Montserrat (a mountain and monastery, filed as a village), Morskie Oko (a lake, filed as a village), Stresa (a town, filed as a village).
- Rows named after a region and a town at once: Dolomites (Cortina), Lago Maggiore (Stresa), Fort William (Glencoe), Cala Gonone (Golfo di Orosei), Ortisei (Val Gardena). A city row can carry an island's appeal or list rank: Palma for Mallorca, Heraklion for Crete.
- The "no outside support" signal is partly a matching gap: Wikivoyage has guides for Ortisei (as St. Ulrich), Malcesine, Montserrat, Stresa, Lagos, Fort William and Cortina, and the Dolomites and Orta's Sacro Monte are World Heritage, but the catalogue rows do not carry them.
