1. Job and audience: one budget traveller, hiker first, turning a window of nights, a home airport and a party into a priced route of two or three towns, then saving it and moving to the days. Started on a phone as often as on a laptop. Mode: Operate.
2. Outcome and proof: a saved route whose total is the same figure on the last wizard step and in the planned receipt, with a day cost on every choice card from the same cost index Explore reads. Proof is the receipt's own lines, each marked "est." where it is an estimate.
3. Direction: the incumbent world. Structural thesis: the money arrives with the first place, the party is asked with the origin (it multiplies every figure), the planned view leads with its receipt, and every view has one primary with the rest secondary or in a "More" menu.
4. Scope: brief V3 items 1 to 10, 12 and 13 inside this surface's files. Anti-goals: no new token or colour, no gradient added, no second Lifestyle control, no flight figure of Carta's, no copy that promises fare search, no visual restyle that belongs to V4 (selected state, caption bands, receipt typesetting).
5. States: cold first run, a mid-wizard reload, a returning planned trip, entitled and guest, the three paths (ready-made, build your own, beds booked), the planned view and the editor; each step's empty, loading and error states stay as they are.
6. Interaction: steps keep their order (seven, row T377-d is the owner's); the footer is one row on a phone; the planned view's footer is Plan your days and Save trip with the rest in More.
7. Open decisions not invented here: the fare-promise copy (T377-c, owner), Booked as a step (T377-d, owner), the cheap tips translation (T377-f, owner), anything in another surface's file (hand-off rows).
