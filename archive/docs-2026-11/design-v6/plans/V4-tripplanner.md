## The changes, in order

1. One selected state (brief V4-1, rule "Selected state"). Every choice shows selection one way: chips and segments `--ink-fill` with `--on-fill`; cards a `2px solid var(--ink)` border and an ink tick. Booked cards (`.guide-booked-bit`, `.guide-mode-check`), trip length chips (`.wtrip-len.on`), the picked-country chip on Trips (`.wready-chip`), the picked trip's tick (`.wtrip-check`), the match card and its Add (`.mcard.on`, `.mcard-add.on`, `.mcard-check`), the quiz chips (`.wq-chip.on`), the country and city pills of the old shortlist (`.guide-country.on`, `.guide-city.on`, `.guide-chip.on`, `.guide-interest.on`, `.guide-country-suggest.on`), the leg mode (`.tleg-mode`), the CountryPickerMap card's Add (`.cpm-card-add.on`, `.cpm-key-sel`). The old rules in files this surface owns (11, 12, 19, 26) are deleted in the same commit as their replacement in 67; selectors that live in files other surfaces own (24, 17, 18, 20) are overridden from 67 (OWNERSHIP rule 5). Selection fills change in `--dur-1` with a reduced-motion branch. Files: `67-v6-tripplanner.css`, `11`, `12`, `19`, `26`, and the JSX only where a tick is drawn in the accent. Check: the walk counts no `--accent-bg` and no accent fill but the view's one primary and the live route on every step.
2. Stop numbers and day buttons in ink (brief V4-1 and rule "Accent marks"). The planned view's stop numbers (`.itin-stop-idx`, accent tint) become ink numerals on `--paper-dim`; the per-day Plan buttons (`.itin-day-plan-btn`, accent tint, a second primary on the sheet) become secondaries. The map's numbered pins stay accent: they are the live route. Files: `12-guided-trip.css`, `67`. Check: the planned view's accent count is the route, its pins and Plan your days.
3. Photographs under captions (brief V4-2, rule "Caption band"). The match card's country name and flag move from the scrim on the photograph into a `--paper` band under it, the scrim is deleted; the trip card's "8 days" badge leaves the photograph for the caption; on a phone a choice card's photograph takes at most a third of the card (trip card 3:1, match card 3:1); the country brief's hero name loses its text shadow and moves under the photo. Files: `CountryMatchCards.jsx`, `ReadyTripsStep.jsx`, `CountryBrief.jsx` (only if the hero needs markup), `67`, the scrim rules deleted from `19-guide-ratings.css` and `12-guided-trip.css`. Check: `grep -n gradient` over 11, 12, 19, 26 and 67 finds no gradient on a photograph; no text-shadow on the surface.
4. The receipt typeset as the receipt (brief V4-3, row T388-j). The total row loses Fraunces: label in `--ui` 14 px, party in `--mono`, the sum in `--mono` 600 with tabular numerals and the figure a person beside it. Opened, the receipt is carta-design's: a cost bar (the shared `CostBar`, read only) over the lines, hairline lines with the figure right-aligned in `--mono` 15 px, the `2px solid var(--ink)` rule above the sum, the sum, and an `--accent-bg` footer with one counterfactual read from the receipt's own lines ("One more night in Rome: +€N", the last stop's beds and days for one night). Files: `TripItinerary.jsx`, `12-guided-trip.css` (the Fraunces rules deleted), `67`, six catalogues (one key under `trip.`). Check: no Fraunces in the receipt; the footer carries a figure that equals the stop's nightly cost.
5. Figures that update in place (brief V4-4, rule "Figure in place"). The wizard band's total, the planned receipt's total and its per-person figure call `useSettledFigure` (shared, read only); the remount trick (`key={total-estBump}`), the `guide-est-bump` and `guide-est-sweep` keyframes and the `.guide-estimate-flash` span go; the delta stays as words in `--mono` beside the figure for three seconds, `--ink` up and `--green` down, without its own animation. The figure's box keeps its width (tabular numerals, `min-width` in `ch`). Files: `GuidedTripWizard.jsx`, `TripItinerary.jsx`, `12-guided-trip.css`, `67`. Check: changing the travellers on the receipt changes the figure with no layout shift (the walk reads the box width before and after); no keyframe named `guide-est-*` left.
6. The route draws itself once (brief V4-5, rule "Route reveal"). TripMap takes an opt-in `revealRoute` prop; on the planned view's first draw the route line grows leg by leg through `setData` on a sliced line, at most `--dur-4` a leg and 900 ms in all, once per open, never on a redraw; under reduced motion (`durationMs` returns 0) the whole route is on the first frame. The day planner and the trip page, which also mount TripMap, do not pass the prop and are unchanged. Files: `src/map/TripMap.jsx`, `TripPlannerTab.jsx`. Check: reduced motion shows the full line on first paint; a second redraw (a travellers change) does not replay.
7. Legs that say what they buy (brief V4-6). Every leg line on the planned view and on Getting there says what its figure includes, in words: "bus fare a person, est." (or "train fare", "ferry fare", "fuel and tolls" for a car leg), so "estimate, ~€44" becomes "~€44 est., train fare a person". Files: `TripItinerary.jsx`, `TravelLegsSection.jsx`, six catalogues. Check: every priced leg line in the walk carries a mode word and an inclusion phrase.
8. Day by day without pills running off screen (brief V4-7, as amended). The shared DayTrack cannot report the shown day back to the planner (no `onSelect`; the trips surface asked shell for it), and the planner's day view drives the map, so the track itself is not built here. Instead the pills become the house day switcher (rule "Day list": "Day 3 of 8, Tue 13 Oct" with Previous and Next secondaries at `--tap`, arrow keys), beside an Overview tab, so no day sits off screen on a phone and every day stays reachable from Your days. Files: `TripItinerary.jsx`, `12-guided-trip.css`, `67`, six catalogues. Check: no horizontal overflow in the tab row at 360 px; Previous and Next move the map's focus stop as the pills did.
9. Match cards that read like an instrument (brief V4-8). The reasons become a run of labelled values: the count in `--mono` through `Intl.NumberFormat` ("1,276"), its label in `--ui` 12 px under it, no bullet; reasons without a count keep their sentence. Files: `CountryMatchCards.jsx`, `67`. Check: no bullet glyph on the card; "1,276" formatted in en, "1.276" in de.
10. The country brief's months in the ink ramp (brief V4-9, rule "Month strip"). Good months `--kind-town` fill with `--on-fill` initials, the traveller's month outlined 2 px `--ink`, the verdict in words in `--ink` (no green or ochre text as the only carrier). Files: `67` (the `.cbrief-mo` rules live in `24-destination-workspace.css`, which this surface does not own: overridden, row T388-c). Check: no accent or accent tint on the strip.
11. The wizard head in one line on a phone (row T388-i). The step name and "Step x of 7" share one row on a phone, the bar under them, so the head drops from 86 px to about 56 px. Files: `67`. Check: fixed chrome at most 170 px on a Pixel 5 on every step; verify_planner_t8's mini rail checks still pass.
12. Press and selection answer at once. Every choice card and chip on the surface gets the shipped touch feedback (`:active` scale 0.97 in `--dur-1`) where it lacks it, hover rules inside `@media (hover: hover)`. Files: `67`. Check: reduced motion branch present.
13. The wizard estimate and an entered fare (row T388-g). Walk a trip with an own fare; if the wizard's running estimate and the receipt differ by the airport transfers, add the transfer line to the estimate through the planner's function. Files: `GuidedTripWizard.jsx` if needed. Check: the two totals agree with an entered fare.
14. Shared element into the planned view (brief V4-10). The picked trip card's photograph has no photograph to land on (the planned top card has none) and the direction allows a non-image shared element only when both sides show the same figures; the trip card shows beds and days, the receipt the whole trip. Not built; written up as open with that reason unless a same-figure pair turns up during the work.
