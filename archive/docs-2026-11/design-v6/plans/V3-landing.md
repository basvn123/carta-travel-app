# V3 plan: the landing page and the first run (surface key `landing`, T382)

Written 2026-10-08 by T382, design wave V3, in planning mode before any app edit. It carries out the V3 list of `docs/design-v6/briefs/landing.md` (part 6) as amended by its Direction check and by `docs/design-v6/DIRECTION.md` (parts 3, 6, 7 and 9). Where this plan and the brief disagree, this plan records why.

## Shape, run on the surface as merged after V2

Impeccable context ran from the root worktree with `--target ../T382-app/src/browse/LandingPage.jsx`. It resolved the app repo as the project root (the target sits in the app's own git tree) and so found neither PRODUCT.md nor DESIGN.md; both were read directly, as the V1 brief did. Shape's interview was not run: the owner told every session of this run to proceed without asking (2026-10-07), so the brief below answers shape's questions from PRODUCT.md, the research, the V1 walk and DIRECTION.md, and marks its assumptions. Direction is refinement inside Carta's world ("Refinement preserves"): no new palette, type or identity.

1. Job and audience. A first-time visitor, most often on a phone and from a shared link or a search, most often without a destination (59 percent, research section 1), counting money first. Mode: Persuade on the landing page, Operate inside the first-run receipt.
2. Outcome and proof. In the first screen they read what a day costs in one real town, with its pedigree, for inputs they can change; then they find a cheap town to walk from, or open the demonstrated one, and the inputs they set travel with them. The proof is the live reprice and the instrument line read from `meta`, never a claim.
3. Selected direction. The receipt is the picture (DIRECTION part 3). Order on a phone: claim, instrument line, town choice, strip, receipt, the one primary, the two shared sentences, the priced list, then proof and coverage. The focal moment stays the reprice.
4. Scope and boundaries. Only the files OWNERSHIP.md gives to `landing`, plus `src/styles/61-v6-landing.css`, one new component (`src/components/ReceiptCard.jsx`, with its OWNERSHIP.md row), the `landing` and `receipt` i18n keys, the two unit test files and a new harness. Anti-goals: no hero photograph, no stat counters, no sticky primary (R5 rejected), no photo toggles (R2 rejected), no change to which visits open on the landing page (T194-a, the owner's), no edit to App.jsx, the shell or the host pages.
5. States. Three demo towns: all measured (Porto), bed measured (Edinburgh), nothing measured (Chamonix). Party 1 to 12 (the shared range), any nights the shared dates hold, six stay tiers of which most towns measure few. A partial catalogue, a missing coverage file, a town that cannot price.
6. Interaction and layout. One set of inputs shared with the app. On a phone everything down to the primary fits the first screen at 380 x 800. From 900 px the claim spans the top and the demonstration and the side column (primary, then the priced list) sit beside each other, so DOM order equals reading order.
7. Constraints. Every figure from the same functions as the destination receipt and the Explore cards. Six languages. `--tap` on a coarse pointer. Tokens only; motion from the tokens with a reduced-motion branch.

## The changes, in order

Each item names the files, the impeccable command whose reference guides it, and how it is checked.

1. One set of inputs (register row T371-c). The landing strip reads and writes the app's shared choices through `useFirstRun()`: people to `group_size`, stay to `stay_tier`, nights to the shared return date from the shared (or Carta's default) arrival. The landing prices with the same `accommodation_model` and cost row the first-run receipt uses, so the two receipts agree to the cent. Files: `src/browse/LandingPage.jsx`, `src/lib/landing.js`. Command: onboard. Check: set one person and a dorm bed, open Porto, and the destination page's receipt prices one person in a dorm bed (harness).
2. One receipt card (register row T209-a). `src/components/ReceiptCard.jsx` draws the head, the lines, the sum, the footers and a slot for one trailing action, plus the shared `ReceiptFigure` (tilde and visible "est."). `FirstRunReceipt.jsx` and `LandingPage.jsx` both render it; the landing's own card rules (`.lp-receipt` and its children in `39-landing.css`) are deleted. Strip labels move to `--ui` 12 px on both strips (a label is not a measured fact). Files: the new component, `FirstRunReceipt.jsx`, `LandingPage.jsx`, `38-first-run.css`, `39-landing.css`, `61-v6-landing.css`, and the OWNERSHIP.md row. Command: extract. Check: one card definition in CSS; unit tests pass.
3. Honest orientation and "each" (register row T371-b). A pure `receiptMix(receipt)` in `src/lib/firstRun.js` names what is measured (all, bed, food, none); the first-run orientation sentence is chosen by it instead of always saying "own rates"; the per-person line carries the tilde whenever the total is an estimate (fixed once, in ReceiptCard). Files: `firstRun.js`, `FirstRunReceipt.jsx`, six `src/i18n/*.js`, `tests/firstRun.test.mjs`. Command: clarify. Check: unit test for the four mixes; harness finds no estimate total without its tilde.
4. One flight sentence, one exclusions sentence. Both receipts print `receipt.flightNone` and `receipt.notIn`; `landing.noFlight`, `landing.notIn` and `landing.orient` are retired from all six catalogues, and the first-run receipt stops borrowing `trip.flightNotPriced`. Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, `FirstRunReceipt.jsx`, six catalogues. Command: clarify. Check: grep finds the retired keys nowhere in `src/`.
5. The phone fold. At 380 x 800 on a cold visit the title, the strip, the receipt sum, its footer line and the primary sit above the bottom nav. Levers, in this order: the primary directly under the receipt and ahead of the two sentences; the orientation folded into the receipt's sub line ("Carta's dates"); the lede replaced by the one instrument line (item 9) rather than stacked with it; the page title at the type scale's 28 px on a phone; "Open {city}" moved into the receipt head (item 6); the stay line's tier word dropped where the strip already shows it (kept when a tier falls back). No sticky primary (R5 rejected). Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, `61-v6-landing.css`, `39-landing.css` (old rules deleted where replaced). Command: layout, adapt. Check: harness at 380 x 800 finds the primary's bottom edge above the bottom nav's top edge.
6. "Open {city}" belongs to the receipt. A secondary inside the card's head, beside the town's name, through ReceiptCard's action slot. Brief V3-6 put it under the sum; the head is chosen because it costs the phone fold nothing and still sits inside the card it opens. Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, `ReceiptCard.jsx`. Check: the button is inside `.frr-card` at both widths.
7. Say what each demo town shows (rule R7, accepted). Each town toggle carries one metadata line read from that town's receipt at the current inputs through `receiptMix`: "All measured", "Bed measured", "Food measured" or "National figures". The accessible name is the town with the line appended. Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, `firstRun.js`, six catalogues, `61-v6-landing.css`. Command: clarify. Check: the three lines match the three receipts' provenance rows (harness).
8. No false choices in the Stay select. A tier the selected town does not measure reads "{tier}, not measured here" in the option text on the landing strip and on the first-run strip; the existing fallback and its sentence stay. A pure `tierMeasured(dest, receiptOpts, tier)` in `firstRun.js` decides it from the same fallback the footer already reads. Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, `FirstRunReceipt.jsx`, `firstRun.js`, six catalogues. Command: harden. Check: in Chamonix every tier but the entire place carries the words (unit test on fixtures, harness on the page).
9. The running instrument (replaces the lede). One line under the title, read from the data: measured beds in N of M places, measured food in F, and the catalogue's date from `meta.generated_at`. No new count is typed. Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, `landing.js` (`catalogueFacts` gains the date), six catalogues. Command: clarify. Check: the line's figures equal the coverage section's figures on the same render (harness).
10. A place for the undecided (rule R1, accepted as the priced list). "Five cheapest towns to walk from": the five towns in the catalogue's `hiking` category with the lowest day cost per person at the current party, stay and Lifestyle, read from the cost index the app already holds (`FirstRunContext.indices`, `computeCosts`), so each figure is the Explore card's figure. Each row: the name in `--ui`, the country, the figure in `--mono` to the cent with its tilde, "est." and the word "national" when a national figure stands in. Rows are buttons that open the town's page: destination pages have no URL of their own yet (no `href` exists for one anywhere in `src/`), so the "real link" part of R1 waits for the shell's navigation rule and is a register row. Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, `landing.js` (pure `cheapestToWalk` with a test), six catalogues, `61-v6-landing.css`. Command: layout. Check: five rows ascending, figures equal `indices.get(id).dayEur`, the list re-ranks when people or stay change (harness).
11. Desktop composition and focus order. From 900 px the claim and the instrument line span the top; under them the demonstration (left) and a side column with the primary, the two sentences and the priced list (right). DOM order is the phone order, which is also the desktop reading order (WCAG 1.3.2, 2.4.3). Files: `LandingPage.jsx`, `61-v6-landing.css`, `39-landing.css`. Command: layout. Check: Tab order follows the visual order; empty band in the first viewport measured and reported.

It waits for the receipt's sum rather than a fixed delay. Command: audit.
13. Reconcile `docs/ONBOARDING_AND_EMPTY_STATES.md` (brief V3-13, register row T194-d). Not done here: the session notes give this session only the landing files, and that root document is not one of them. It stays a register row for the docs owner.
