## Motion thesis (animate.md)

Focal moment: the reprice. When the traveller changes the Lifestyle, every day cost on the screen and the count settle in place together through `settleFigure()`, the one dip the house rule allows; that is the page answering. Continuity: the card photograph becomes the destination page's photograph (the shared element already shipped), the preview rises 4 px beside its card, the map frame crossfades from its still placeholder. Feedback: the press state already shipped in `02-base.css` (scale 0.98, opacity) is the only press; hover is a border colour at `--dur-1`; nothing lifts. Budget: one Web Animations call per changed figure, CSS opacity elsewhere; nothing loops and nothing moves on scroll.

## The changes, in order

1. Cards that read as a price list with pictures. Every grid photograph is 3:2 from 769 px, 16:10 on the phone two-up; the mosaic keeps two sizes instead of four (metros, the top tier and landscapes span 4 of 12, everything else 3), so a 1280 x 800 filtered screen shows at least eight priced cards. The seal leaves the photograph on rails and on a grid already filtered to a tier. Card names take the card size (Fraunces 19 px), rail titles the section size (22 / 26 px), the h1 the page title (28 / 40 px). Files: ExploreTab.jsx (spanFor, seal), ExploreRails.jsx (seal), 62-v6-explore.css. Check: euro figures inside the first filtered screen at 1280 counted by the scratch walk, 8 or more.
2. The preview as a popover (explore N6, house "Dialog family"). A `--bg-card` popover with a `--rule` border, 10 px radius and `--shadow-2`, at most 320 px wide, beside the card where the column has room, else under it; it never covers the photograph. It no longer repeats the name and score the card already shows; it keeps the rank, the role word, what the place is known for, the sights, one length-of-stay line, the reach line, a twelve-cell month strip (the shared MonthStrip, best months filled, the chosen month outlined 2 px `--ink`) and the compact receipt with a thin cost bar above its lines (bed and food in the ink ramp, 8 px, no figures on the bar). Opens on hover after 150 ms, on focus after 400 ms, on the info control at once; Escape closes it. It arrives in `--dur-1` with opacity and a 4 px rise, none under reduced motion; the old ink panel and its row cascade are overridden. Files: ExploreTab.jsx, CostSummary.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css, i18n under `explore.*`. Check: the walk measures 0 px² of photo overlap at 1280 and on the phones, and verify_explore's preview checks pass (updated with a comment where they read the old panel).
3. Spend the accent once. Filter chips `--paper-dim` with `--ink` and a `--rule` border; "See all" an `--ink` underlined link; the gauge's dearest band `--ink` (explore N7); the side "Clear all" `--ink`; a selected card a 2 px `--ink` border, not the accent; the open preview's info control the selected state (`--ink-fill`); fold counts the count chip. Explore's own Lifestyle cell leaves the input row now that the settings line carries Lifestyle on both widths (rows T381-b, T383-a in part). Files: ExploreTab.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css. Check: on the idle grid the only accent is the active nav, the cheapest pin when the map is up, and focus rings.
4. Motion that states change. The count settles through `useSettledFigure`; every card and rail figure settles when its value changes (a Lifestyle change), never on mount; the grid dims to 0.6 while a filter change is pending (`useDeferredValue`, opacity at `--dur-2`, no spinner); active chips enter with opacity and scale from 0.9 in `--dur-1` (leaving needs FilterChips, the shell's: register row); the map canvas crossfades in over `--dur-3` once the style has loaded; the hover lift, the photo zoom and the `box-shadow` transitions of cards and rail cards are retired for a `--dur-1` border colour inside `@media (hover: hover)`. Every rule has a reduced-motion branch. Files: ExploreTab.jsx, ExploreMap.jsx, CostSummary.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css. Check: a grep of the v6 sheet finds no literal duration, no `transition: all`, no animated `box-shadow`, `width`, `height`, `top` or `left`; the detector is clean.
5. Pins that feel live. A pin that becomes the cheapest in view scales from 0.9 in `--dur-1` (the individual `scale` property, which composes with the positioning transform); one class per render, so two pins are never emphasised at once. Labels collide no more (row T383-c): labels are placed in priority order (the cheapest first, then the keyboard layer's order) and a label that would overlap a placed one stays a dot, still a keyboard stop. Files: ExploreMap.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css. Check: the walk at zoom 8 near Venice counts 0 overlapping labels and exactly one cheapest pin; verify_keyboard explore-map keeps its stops.
6. Rails with position (house "Rails and cuts"). The rail head shows "1 to 4 of 12" in `--mono`, updated as the strip scrolls; Left and Right move focus card to card inside a strip; cards snap to their start. Files: ExploreRails.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css, i18n (`rail.position`). Check: a keyboard walk pages a rail without a mouse.
7. Imagery consistency. Rail cards take the same 3:2 as the grid; a missing photo keeps the flag-and-name placeholder; `sizes` and srcset stay (verify_explore's width check). Files: ExploreRails.jsx, 62-v6-explore.css. Check: no row mixes ratios at 1280.
8. One small floor fix in a file this surface owns: the compact receipt's "Change" control (CostSummary, drawn on the destination page) gets the `--tap` box on a coarse pointer (row T385-h, its CostSummary part). File: 62-v6-explore.css.
