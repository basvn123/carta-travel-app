## What the new design system must solve

From the critique and the audit together; each line is a requirement on T419 to T424.

1. **One selected state** that is not a near-black slab, used the same way by nav, tabs, segments, chips, tiles and choice cards. Solid fill stays for the one primary action.
2. **Separate colour roles**: action, focus (never reading as an error), rating, data status, destructive. Every tint gets a named text colour that clears 4.5:1, checked by a script.
3. **Four button kinds** (primary, secondary, quiet, danger) plus icon button and one unmistakable disabled style.
4. **One `<Money>`** with fixed slots (estimate marker, amount, unit, basis, source) and the context rule: whole euros on cards, cents on receipts. One estimate notation (below).
5. **One `<Score>` and one verdict seal**, legible: 11 px floor for text, 12 px for figures.
6. **One badge and tag family** for tier, editorial, time and meta labels.
7. **One chip** spec for filter, choice, category and summary chips.
8. **One card anatomy**: media, caption, title, meta, price, rating, actions.
9. **Scales as tokens**: type (with line heights and a 68 ch measure), radius, elevation, z-index layers, alpha, spacing with one 16 px gutter, 4 named breakpoints.
10. **One stepper/progress** for both planners, and **one view toggle** (List/Map, same label on phone and desktop).
11. **One page shell** for destination, region, journey, itinerary and the five item pages.
12. **One date format** per context.
13. **Keep and promote** the receipt, the provenance microcopy, the fact grid, tabular mono figures and the greyscale-safe ink ramps.
14. **Enforcement**: lint rules for font size, radius, z-index, rgba, raw buttons, money literals and duplicate selectors.
15. **CSS per route** so the system stops feeding one 868 KB render-blocking stylesheet (fits T420 and the migration tasks).

## Decision: one estimate notation (closes T417-c)

`~€87 est.` The tilde joins the amount with no space, "est." follows the amount as its own word, and the unit comes after: `~€87 est. a day`, `~€556.48 est.`. It keeps the landing promise ("a tilde and est.") and PRODUCT.md's rule word for word, and retires `~ €x` (space), `est. €x` (prefix), `€x est.` (no tilde) and a bare `~€x`. `<Money est>` renders it; no string writes it by hand.
